## Implementation and integration

- `server/cosmetic-*` shares the existing shop ownership, acquisition, wallet, reward and commercial fulfillment services. A cosmetic ID remains bound to its original type and permanent gameplay card where applicable. Single variants, themed/evolution/Kindred-wide bundles, rewards and direct sandbox cosmetic Checkout grant licenses only.
- `src/player/Cosmetics.tsx` provides an authenticated, paginated gallery, creature/Kindred/theme/ownership filters, original/alternate comparison, purchase links into the established shop confirmation, equip/restore and customization controls. Existing artwork navigation and equipment remain compatible. The original and alternate use the same full-bleed renderer and printed information, including rarity.
- `cosmetic_preferences` stores optimistic revisions and the selected back, field and treatment. Changes survive refresh/restart/deck changes. Conflicting writes fail instead of overwriting another device. Trading the last playable copy preserves the license/equipment preference but suppresses use until reacquisition. Cosmetic trading remains disabled.
- `starting_cosmetic_grant` records immutable, idempotent original-Kindred entitlements through the normal acquisition ledger. New-player grants share the starting-collection transaction. Existing-player claims and reviewed administrator backfills preserve existing preferences. Concurrent claims do not duplicate grants. An already-owned paid license keeps its provenance and does not consume an unneeded free slot; a legitimate later refund can expose the still-unused free entitlement.
- `match_cosmetic_presentation` freezes personal presentation for new matches. Existing matches retain neutral defaults. Buying, equipping, archiving or refunding during a match never rewrites the deck, game state, configuration, score or appearance snapshot. Historical artwork endpoints authorize only that player's current ownership or archived match references.
- The shared card renderer adds static metallic, holographic or decorative edge treatments. They do not obscure full-card art, change statistics or add continuous animation. Selected backs appear at the player's hand origin; the opponent uses a neutral representative concealed back without any hidden card/deck metadata. Personal fields sit behind the unchanged tile layout. Saved neutral-board mode and standardized-art formats suppress cosmetic presentation without confiscating ownership.
- `shop-features.ts`, shop quotes and the storefront use database time for availability, feature schedules, section/order, promotions and announcements. Feature removal retains ordinary catalog availability. Audience-qualified promotion discounts replace rather than stack the ordinary discount. Comparison prices derive from the same product's ordinary price and exact remaining contents; arbitrary old prices are rejected. Expired/changed confirmations fail before charging, including promotions that round to the same integer price. Existing purchase limits and immutable settled terms remain authoritative.
- Protected Shop administration adds cosmetic definition/asset/theme/style controls, starting-policy digest review, per-player backfill previews and featured/promotion scheduling. The existing validated JSON editor handles exact assignments, announcements, product contents, availability and limits. Save/publication/version history/rollback remain explicit. Future unsupported cosmetic types cannot be published or sold.

The cosmetic-only quote and commercial snapshots now contain no gameplay card pool. This also fixes a misleading product details panel that previously listed founding cards for a cosmetic product. Direct sandbox Checkout accepts implemented CSS treatments without inventing an image asset; image cosmetics retain their image/approval/integrity checks.

## Security, failure and recovery

Authentication, CSRF/origin checks, global/per-operation limits and administrator role checks remain in force. Equipment and image access are scoped to the authenticated account. The gallery hides unpublished/unsupported/sandbox-only definitions from ineligible players. Wrong slots/types, unowned variants, absent playable cards, stale preference revisions and stale grant previews fail closed. Asset publication checks registry approval/category, portable path, bytes, checksum, media signature, sanitized SVG, aspect ratio and size; the repository importer/build validator also decodes raster pixels and verifies source masters.

Automated cases exercise concurrent grants/preferences, earning and purchasing licenses, partial bundle ownership, real evolution and thirty-card artwork bundles, cross-account access, product archival, original-source wallet compensation, direct simulated commercial fulfillment replay, dispute holds and ledger reconciliation. Restricted/refunded licenses cannot remain equipped in current collection/new matches; historical snapshots remain independently auditable. Original playable inventory is unchanged by cosmetic grants/refunds.

Image load failure gives a neutral back/field or the existing card fallback. A neutral-board preference preserves geometry. Treatments use static pseudo-elements with no flashing, looping animation or full-face compositing. Browser tests check system reduced motion, portrait/landscape touch and all five desktop viewport targets.

Back up the source bundle, immutable artwork registry/masters, configuration publication history and database using the established operator process. Restore into isolation and verify preservation first. Repair an image from its exact historical committed revision or publish a new revision; never overwrite immutable bytes or reset player assets to repair presentation.
