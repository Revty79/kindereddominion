## Backup and recovery

Use `sudo kindred-backup` for a hosted backup and retain its verified dump, source bundle, immutable asset manifest/revisions, image/deployment receipt and private configuration securely. The updater takes and verifies its own consistent backup and separate restoration before live migration. Back up original artwork masters with the committed source/artwork revision; browser storage is never the only production copy.

On failure, preserve the complete journal, log, backup and current database. Inspect `sudo kindred-status`. A build/test failure before downtime leaves the running app intact. A failure after quiescing but before migration attempts the compatible old application. Once migration uncertainty is recorded, do not force-start old images, delete journals or restore an old dump over newer player data. After investigation, the optional `sudo kindred-update --resume` retries the exact recorded eligible revision with fresh backup/rehearsal. It is not a routine update step. Compatible image rollback and separate-database recovery remain explicit advanced operations in [V1.5 operations](V1.5-OPERATIONS.md#failure-and-recovery). Operator-only recovery is `sudo kindred-update --recover-operator` after reviewing the retained trusted bundle. Do not prune images, volumes, worktrees or databases to bypass a failed safeguard.
