## Immediate placement

1. Select a card. Move the pointer over legal tiles to see tactical effects, or hold a dragged card over a tile. Keyboard focus also previews a destination.
2. Click/tap a legal destination, or release a drag there: **the move submits immediately**. There is no placement confirmation dialog or mode selector.
3. Use Escape, **Clear selection**, another hand card, or a cancelled drag before committing. A committed move has no undo.
4. Illegal destinations explain their restriction and do not spend a card or advance the turn. Slow/rejected/uncertain requests retain server validation and saved-match recovery.
5. Placement, opponent actions and abilities animate after the server accepts them. Choose Display for Normal, Fast, Reduced motion, Instant or the device preference. These settings affect presentation only.
6. Use Last Move for recorded player/opponent consequences, replay/steps, final result and immediate Current game return. **HISTORY — NOT LIVE** and recorded-power labels distinguish that view. Old unsupported details are honestly unavailable.
