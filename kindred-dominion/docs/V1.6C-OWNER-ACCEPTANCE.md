## Controls

- **Click/tap:** select a playable hand card, move over the battlefield and choose a tile marked Legal placement. The original hand position remains recognizable. The floating portrait is offset from the pointer and target.
- **Drag:** press a hand card, move it onto a legal tile and release. On touchscreens, drag directly on the card; swipe the hand rail below cards to browse the rest of the hand.
- **Review first:** the default hand-header mode. Choosing a tile opens the existing server preview. Review it and choose **Confirm placement**.
- **Place directly:** select this hand-header mode for click/tap or drag release to submit immediately. The server still validates and saves the action.
- **Cancel:** Escape, Clear selection, Cancel placement, clicking the selected card again, or dropping outside a legal tile/returning it to the hand. Another card replaces the current selection. Focus loss and touch cancellation also clear uncommitted gestures.
- **Keyboard:** focus a hand card and press Enter/Space. An arrow key focuses its first legal tile. Arrow keys navigate tiles; Home/End go to the row edges. Enter/Space chooses the focused tile. Review mode requires the normal Confirm placement button. Escape cancels.
- **Inspect:** use Inspect beside a hand or battlefield card. Inspection never places the card.

While checking with the server, placement controls are locked and the saved board remains visible. A rejected placement preserves the card. After an interrupted response, review the reloaded saved position before trying another action. **Reload saved match** is available in the error notice; reloading the page and resuming is also safe.
