## Transactions, probabilities and recovery

Purchases require an account-owned, short-lived exact quote. Settlement locks the player and current publication selections, rechecks ownership, lifetime history, currency, availability, limits and immutable terms, then atomically debits currency, grants assets and records receipts. Client prices, seeds, currency amounts and outcomes are rejected. Same-key/same-quote retries recover a purchase; changed terms need new confirmation. Insufficient funds and an actual late PostgreSQL trigger failure leave no partial debit or grants.

First-copy products exclude both currently owned and lifetime-acquired identities. Five-card boosters use server cryptographic randomness, distinct selections and exact basis-point rarity intervals. Conditional selection protects strict later slots; unavailable strict guarantees disable a pack. Explicit fallback slots renormalize available rarities when weighted pools are depleted or reserved. Full and conditional probabilities/counts and fallback policies are shown before purchase. Reduced final packs disclose omitted slots, smaller contents and prorated integer price. Zero eligible cards cannot be purchased.

Card results and ownership settle before opening. Persistent reveal progress is monotone and presentation only. Browser interruption, application restart, repeated reveal and skipped animation cannot grant again. Local confirmation storage is keyed by player and contains no credentials; server receipts and pack history are the authoritative recovery source.

First-copy, starter, administrator and sandbox assets remain bound. Additional-copy products are explicit. Administrator-funded purchases cannot launder cards into tradeable purchased lots. Genuine purchased funding can impose the configured holding period, retaining original currency lot links for later commercial investigation. D itself has no checkout or external payment dependency.

Complete wallet compensation reverses only the original unconsumed/unreserved/unrestricted acquired lots, retains lifetime first-copy history, returns the exact original currency origins and records immutable return lineage. Reserved, transferred or consumed assets stop for investigation. Free rewards and commercial orders cannot create wallet refunds through this endpoint. Cosmetic revocation targets the original current acquisition; a later legitimate reacquisition cannot be revoked by an old receipt. Archived match artwork remains available through its frozen reference.
