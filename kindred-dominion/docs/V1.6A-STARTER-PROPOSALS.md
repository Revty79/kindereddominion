# V1.6A - Forty-copy starting collection proposals

**Owner review required before hosted activation.** Both new-player and existing-account activation default to false. This is a 40-copy collection, containing 30 unique identities and ten additional bound copies. Standard battle decks remain 15 cards with the existing card/game-mode limits.

All 150 executable cards were evaluated for placement, influence, power, actual effects, evolution dependencies, copy limits and early/late use: [complete card evaluation](v1.6a/CARD-EVALUATION.md), [full mechanical evaluation data](v1.6a/CARD-EVALUATION.json). Rarity, names and artwork were not the selection rule.

Recipe version: `founding-40-proposal-v1`. Exact proposal digest: `6420f2e5ee092fed9e09f7d7efcd81cfd9608b3e24fea8e5b4be2e0e9023cee3`. This digest identifies the definitions, not an owner approval.

Every Kindred receives one copy of each of its original 30 founders, plus the ten rows below. Each selected identity receives one extra copy. Purchased/earned copies remain additional to this grant. Earlier recipients do not receive another ten merely because a new recipe version is published.

## Review and activation boundaries

These decks deliberately repeat foundational cards and retain usable development/evolution routes. They do not promise equal AI win rates or human balance. [Repeatable balance results](v1.6a/BALANCE.json) and [actual turn-by-turn development metrics](v1.6a/DEVELOPMENT-METRICS.json) are reported separately. Existing high-power efficiency, continuous enhancement/weakening and death/resurrection combinations must be distinguished from defects introduced by these starting quantities.

The protected Starter collections panel can edit versions, quantities and all three decks, validate/publish an inactive proposal, show exact ownership deltas, enable DEV grants, and record owner approval before hosted activation. Historical grants retain the exact selected configuration and content snapshot. Unknown/conflicting original Kindreds are blocked for authorized review. Backfill never replaces any existing deck.
