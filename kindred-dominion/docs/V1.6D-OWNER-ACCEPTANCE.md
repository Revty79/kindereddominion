## Read a preview

1. Begin or resume a saved match and select a card. Legal tiles use the existing validated list; unavailable tiles identify enemy/unowned territory, occupancy, required tokens, hosts or evolution prerequisites.
2. Move over a tile, focus it with the keyboard, or target it with a finger. A stable target produces a debounced server preview. **PATTERN** shows printed influence reach. **PREVIEW** marks actual known consequences.
3. Read **T** for placement tokens, **P** for effective power and **F** for fortification. Other symbols include CAP territory, X destruction, SH protection, R replacement, EV evolution, REV resurrection, RET return and EX banishment. These values remain separate from current tile values and printed card statistics.
4. Use **Preview details**, or **Consequences** after choosing a review destination, for tile-by-tile changes, conditional ability explanations, ordered effects and scoring. **IF** marks a conditional ability. Expanded details are optional.
5. An uncertainty note means later effects resolve after placement. Private draws, hand generation, random targets, private-history conditions and long chains are never presented as guaranteed. The score is for the known preview before the computer chooses its response.
6. Confirm in **Review first**, or use **Place directly** for immediate legal click/drop submission. The server validates the action again. Invalid targets submit no gameplay action.

With a selected card, arrows focus/navigate tiles; Home/End move to row edges. Enter/Space selects a tile. Escape, Clear selection, invalid/outside drops and relevant pointer/touch interruption cancel. The floating portrait may move beside the affected group or back over the hand area to keep important tiles visible. Leaving an unchosen hover target clears its preview; a deliberately chosen review destination remains until confirmation or cancellation.
