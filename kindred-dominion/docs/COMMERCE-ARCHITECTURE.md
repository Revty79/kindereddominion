# Sandbox commercial architecture

F.1 separates versioned products, immutable confirmations/orders, provider mappings/events/payments, fulfillment and source-asset relationships. Stripe is the first genuine sandbox adapter, implemented in F.2/F.3; provider-specific API objects must not become the application's ownership ledger. The interface can later support another authorized provider without treating platform receipts as card definitions or changing permanent collectible identities. No mobile purchase, physical product, redemption or additional provider is implemented now.

Commercial activation has independent boundaries: the server mode, a reviewed published catalog and a permanently designated sandbox account. Production refuses an active commercial mode. Every current product/order is sandbox-only and ineligible for live sale; an administrator cannot flip a live-payment flag. Quotes/orders grant nothing. Authenticated actor ownership, CSRF, exact quote/configuration references, authoritative integer minor-unit prices, request fingerprints and ordered player locks protect creation. A replay returns its original order, including when a second request key refers to the same quote. Stale publication/amount-integrity checks require a fresh review or investigation.

Aether packages are provisional USD499/999/1999/4999/9999cents for500/1000/2100/5500/11500units. Their effective unit rates differ. Fixed sets reference accepted additional-copy shop definitions and clearly include already owned cards. Ordinary Gold first-copy paths remain intact. Cash single-card targets inherited from D conflict with its Aether price table; they are not silently generated as live or approved products. Review that relationship before publishing a genuine test-provider catalog, and keep any later policy choice explicit. No simulated amount is actual revenue.

A published commercial product snapshot pins the economy, shop and content versions, exact associated definitions and card identities. Player-facing confirmations omit the internal full shop configuration. Component fulfillment will reuse `fulfillShopAssets`, central currency movement and the existing immutable acquisition/pack/cosmetic services, producing commercial order links rather than a second inventory. First-copy shop definitions cannot be substituted for commercial additional-copy products. Alternate artwork continues through the existing approved asset pipeline. Randomized sandbox components remain live-ineligible pending separate policy/provider review.

Order creation, external Checkout creation, verified payment recording and fulfillment are distinct recovery stages. Provider requests must use stable order-specific idempotency; network failure must retain an investigable order rather than holding a database transaction open across an external call. The payment adapter must verify account/environment/order/session/amount/currency and line-item identity. A browser success return is not proof of payment. An immutable verified payment may remain pending fulfillment after a failed asset transaction; fulfillment must grant all components exactly once or roll back the entire grant, retaining the verified payment for recovery.

Provider events retain unique account-scoped identifiers, a minimal normalized snapshot/digest and auditable processing state. Raw credentials, card data and unnecessary customer information must not enter logs or application records. Notifications remain post-commit and recoverable. Refund/dispute stages retain original receipts and payment-linked source ancestry. Safe reversal is bounded to explicitly selected original assets under a reviewed sandbox policy; spent, transferred, reserved or ambiguous cases require audited investigation, not unrelated confiscation. Those fulfillment/refund workflows are F.3/F.4 work, not F.1 claims.

Restoration checks commercial snapshot/quote/order relationships and verified payment/fulfillment references in addition to the established wallet/ownership/trade audits. Tables and source history are retained; no historical commercial purchases are fabricated for existing players. Backup/rehearsal/updater boundaries remain unchanged.

SDK/API integration and owner credential instructions are delivered in the following submilestones; genuine Stripe acceptance remains separate from deterministic provider simulations.
