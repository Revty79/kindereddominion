## Ordinary-player trading guide

1. Open **Trading Hall → My tradeable cards**. Eligible copies can be offered. Starter/first-copy grants stay with your account. Reserved copies belong to an open offer; waiting-period, payment-review or account restrictions can temporarily prevent other copies from being offered. Exact collectible copies are selected separately by edition/serial.
2. To advertise a card, open **My listings → Create public listing**, click its artwork, enter a quantity, optionally select wanted cards and save. A listing does not reserve or transfer anything.
3. Use **Find cards** to choose a desired card, or **Public listings** to find a player who is offering it. A direct offer can start by searching the player's name. Their private collection is not disclosed. They can accept only copies they are eligible to exchange.
4. Click the cards you want and the eligible cards you will give; adjust quantities and add more cards as needed. Inspect placement, influence and abilities using the existing enlarged card inspection. Choose an expiration, then **Review offer**.
5. Review the named player, both sides and expected ownership changes. Pay attention to deck warnings. **Confirm trade** submits the exact terms and reserves the submitting player's eligible copies; it does not transfer ownership yet. A saved private draft reserves nothing.
6. Review incoming offers. Choose **Accept offer**, **Make counteroffer** or **Decline offer**. The final review requires explicit confirmation. A changed offer needs fresh consent. The server rechecks current ownership, restrictions, quantities and the exact version; both sides settle together or neither side changes.
7. The completed receipt records quantities at settlement. If a deck needs attention, use its repair link, edit the deck and save a legal formation. Existing matches retain their original authoritative cards.
8. Use **Wish list** for additional copies you want. Wishes are private unless explicitly made public, and are completed explicitly. Mutual matches never send an offer automatically.

For a specific limited collectible, its owner can start a private direct offer and choose the exact owned edition/serial. To let other players discover and request one, use **My specific collectibles → List this collectible**. This shares only that edition, serial and disclosed contents. It does not expose the rest of the collection or reserve supply. Publicly listed collectibles are selectable visually; no UUID entry is required. Another copy can never be substituted without renewed consent. Unsupported, bound, held or policy-disabled cosmetic trading stays unavailable.

An interrupted confirmation remains recoverable on that browser under the same account. Retry the saved action after an uncertain connection result; its original idempotency key is retained. A stale version is discarded and current terms are shown for a new review. Confirmed trades cannot be undone through a player action.

## Privacy, integrity and implementation

- Incoming/outgoing/history filtering occurs on the server before pagination. Private drafts remain visible only to their author. Incoming counts exclude unsubmitted drafts and expired actions.
- Ownership presentation derives from authoritative inventory and the existing provenance/reservation checks. Categories avoid double-counting and never manufacture eligible copies. The original detailed records remain available to authorized administration; player explanations avoid acquisition-lot terminology.
- New collectible discovery is opt-in. Another player's unlisted collection, acquisition history, payment details and original owner records are never returned by discovery. Blocked, restricted, sandbox or ineligible sellers and unavailable instances are excluded.
- Two additive metadata tables retain nonbinding collectible listings and immutable revision history. The existing listing cap is shared across ordinary and collectible advertisements. Creation/closure retain the existing request ledger, owner authorization, rate limits and idempotency checks.
- Actual offer consent, inventory reservations, provenance ancestry, exact-instance holds and atomic transfer services remain authoritative. A successful exact transfer also closes the old discovery listing and appends its history in the same transaction. No additional collectible is issued by listing or trading.
- Cosmetic previews use the immutable original shop/artwork reference and existing checksum/format/SVG validation. Access requires ownership/history, a consented offered collectible or an eligible opt-in public listing. Private artwork is not exposed merely because someone guesses a UUID.
- Ordinary card listings can receive offers containing eligible specific collectibles as well as regular card quantities. The listing's requested card, revision and live availability checks remain intact.
- Default card/edition tradeability, account thresholds, holding periods, commercial safeguards and all permanent founding identities are unchanged. Reduced thresholds and eligible reward fixtures exist only in explicitly registered disposable tests; starter grants remain bound.
- Legacy player routes remain supported, including catalog, packs, match history and artwork. The new challenges destination uses existing authoritative objective services. Administration and Studio retain independent server authorization and are outside ordinary player navigation.
