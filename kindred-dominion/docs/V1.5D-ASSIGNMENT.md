# Kindred Dominion — V1.5D
## Card Shop, Booster Packs, Rarity, Collection Acquisition and Cosmetic Economy

Repository: `Revty79/kindereddominion`

Implement V1.5D of Kindred Dominion using the accepted V1.5C core economy as its foundation.

Kindred Dominion is intended to become a sustainable commercial collectible tactical card game.

This milestone creates the in-game shop, collectible acquisition systems, configurable pack products, premium cosmetic ownership and expansion-ready commercial infrastructure.

No live payment processing is authorized during this milestone.

## 2. Approved collectible principles

Preserve:

- The 150 founding card identities.
- The five founding Kindreds.
- Existing original artwork.
- Existing gameplay definitions.
- Existing permanent collectible IDs.
- Existing deck rules and match mechanics.

Ordinary Gold-funded acquisition generally grants first copies.

Additional playable copies can be obtained through premium purchases, tournaments, advanced achievements, mastery rewards and future trading.

Future premium-exclusive gameplay collections are authorized as a design capability, but no new paid-exclusive gameplay cards are to be published during this milestone without owner approval.

## 7. First-copy duplicate protection

Implement first-copy duplicate protection for ordinary Gold-funded founding boosters.

When generating an ordinary first-copy pack:

- Identify eligible cards the player has not previously acquired through the lifetime first-copy mechanism.
- Exclude already-owned cards.
- Apply configured rarity and collection eligibility.
- Prevent duplicate card identities within the same first-copy pack.
- Update eligibility as cards are selected.
- Atomically grant the resulting cards.

Do not grant additional playable copies through ordinary first-copy packs.

Additional copies are acquired through explicitly authorized duplicate reward or purchase products.

When fewer eligible cards remain than the normal pack size, provide a clearly disclosed reduced-size purchase or another equivalent, explicitly priced completion option.

Do not charge the full five-card price for fewer cards without an explicitly disclosed and accepted product rule.

Show the actual eligible card count and effective price before confirmation.

When no eligible cards remain, disable the first-copy product for that player.

Prevent future trading from allowing repeated acquisition of free first copies.

Lifetime first-copy history and tradeability policies must preserve the economic integrity of the system.

## 10. Complete Kindred collection purchases

Implement fixed-content collection bundles.

Each founding Kindred contains exactly 30 unique gameplay cards.

Support:

- Complete Nature collection.
- Complete Mythical collection.
- Complete Arcane collection.
- Complete Undead collection.
- Complete Constructs collection.
- Complete founding collection bundle.

Provide distinct product behavior for:

### Collection completion

Grant one copy of each eligible missing card.

Display the missing contents and actual price before purchase.

Do not charge players for already-owned cards unless the product explicitly grants additional copies.

### Additional complete collection

Grant one additional playable copy of every card contained in the purchased collection.

For example, an existing Mythical player may purchase an additional complete Mythical collection and receive 30 additional playable cards.

Clearly distinguish these product types.

Include configurable bundle discounts and pricing rules.

Support fixed-content future expansion bundles.
