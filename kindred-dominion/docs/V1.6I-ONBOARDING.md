## New-player guide

1. Register or sign in, then open **My Collection**. Compare all five Kindreds and inspect their sample cards. Confirm the permanent starting choice once.
2. Inspect the collection and its quantities. An active expanded starter grants 40 playable copies across 30 identities; the Standard battle deck contains 15 cards.
3. Open **Learn to play**. Follow the lessons or skip optional guidance. Try the reward-free board: select a card, preview a tile and click/tap a legal destination. The full battle also supports dragging.
4. Inspect evolution/replacement prerequisites and the difference between placement tokens, power and scored points. Use the detailed rule section for ability conditions and limits.
5. Open **Deck Builder**. Review or edit the recommended deck and save a legal formation. Drafts remain editable but cannot start an ordinary battle.
6. Open **Play**, choose a legal saved deck and scoring mode, and begin an ordinary computer battle. Use Last Move for inspection/replay and Display for animation preferences.
7. Check **Progression** and **Challenges** for actual completion, claim availability and the published participation requirements. Practice, replay and simply acknowledging instructions do not substitute for legitimate battle evidence.
8. In **Card Shop**, review the product, its exact eligible cards, quantities and unavailable reasons. Guaranteed-new first copies and additional playable copies serve different purposes. Equip supported owned appearances through **Customization**.
9. Open **Trading Hall** when eligible. Starter/first-copy grants remain bound; other copies can be held, reserved or restricted. Review both exact sides before consenting.

The tutorial remains available through navigation after dismissal or completion. Checklist navigation is immediate; a delayed preference save cannot override a later navigation choice. Saved learning progress survives application restart and another browser login. A conflicting device edit requires reloading guide progress rather than silently overwriting it.
