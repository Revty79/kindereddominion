`trading-hall.ts` and `trading-hall-routes.ts` provide authenticated public card/name/Kindred/rarity/collection discovery, own listing management, public wishes, private personal wishes, public identity search, advisory matching and conversion into a direct exact offer. Listings are nonbinding and reserve nothing. The viewer who submits a concrete offer reserves only its own offered side; the listing owner must explicitly accept the immutable reciprocal terms. Source listing/revision and public interest are frozen with the submitted version; edited, closed or unavailable listings cannot silently change a confirmation or reserve the viewer's cards. Replays recover the original request rather than creating duplicate offers.

One active interest listing per owner/card can aggregate quantity and multiple wanted identities. Quantities are rechecked against eligible lots; publicly visible availability is capped at the quantity the owner chose to list. Wants guide negotiation rather than imposing rarity/value equivalence. Counteroffers remain independent renewed consent. Listing caps are configurable; the new wish cap defaults to 500 with backward-compatible parsing of prior published policies. No existing configuration is automatically replaced.

Wishes default private. Desired quantities mean **additional copies**, so current ownership does not silently complete a wish. Completion is explicit and archived. Matching may use the viewer's own private wish, but only another player's public listing/wish interest. It never automatically submits or settles a trade. Public views expose only public identity and voluntarily published interest, not email, private collection totals, source lots, private wishes or commercial records. Blocks, restrictions, sandbox state and current eligibility filter discovery. Metadata mutations require ownership, strict input validation, optimistic revisions, bounded request rates and idempotency.

Owners can still close listings or complete wishes during global suspension, including unavailable/retired personal entries. History remains intact. Public search/pages are bounded; unavailable nonbinding entries may reduce a page's visible count, with the next-page indicator reflecting remaining scanned candidates. Listing/wish activity creates no cards or currency.
