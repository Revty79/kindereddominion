# V1.6H economy and profitability review

Verified implementation / provisional planning assumptions, 2026-11-27. No live payments, observed sales, approved final prices, accounting policy or retention measurements are implied. The owner controls final commercial decisions.

## Reproduce and adjust

Run `npx tsx scripts/store-model.mts` to regenerate [STORE-MODEL.json](v1.6h/STORE-MODEL.json) and [INTEGRATED-CATALOG.json](v1.6h/INTEGRATED-CATALOG.json). Run with `--check` for a deterministic check (normalizing Windows checkout line endings). For another scenario use `--input=.local/store-assumptions.json`; the object accepts `settings` and `finance` overrides validated by the same schemas as protected administration. Do not use `--check` with different assumptions against the baseline artifact. No database, credentials or player grants are involved.

Administrators can open Shop > Store reporting and balancing > Open store insights > Load and run store model. Adjust inputs and rerun; download the complete result. These are temporary scenarios. Save reviewed assumptions through Commerce > configuration > store planning fields, validate, save, give a reason and publish a version. This never changes settled receipts, existing matches or currency balances.

## Monthly commercial projection

Assumptions: 5% paying conversion, $15 gross spending/payer/month, two Checkouts/payer ($7.50 mean Checkout), browser fee 2.9% + $0.30/order, refunds 2% of sales, disjoint disputes 0.2% of sales plus $15 per disputed order. These are adjustable model inputs, **not fee quotes**. Monthly fixed assumptions are $30 hosting, $200 artwork/content and $100 other operations. This static cost example omits scaling costs, taxes, unpaid labor and other owner expenses unless entered. Do not extrapolate the large-community row as a business forecast.

Break-even under these assumptions: 25 paying players, about 487 MAU. At zero conversion the model reports no attainable break-even, rather than dividing by zero. Refund plus dispute sales cannot exceed 100%; positive sales require a positive Checkout frequency. Category allocation must total exactly 10,000 basis points.

Gross sales is the modeled provider consideration once. Net sales deducts modeled refunded/disputed sales; cash receipts also deducts processing and dispute fees. Contribution subtracts modeled fixed costs. Aether spending is redemption/use of already acquired units and is **not another sale**. Illustrative recognition uses a configurable 75% redemption assumption; this is neither actual recognized revenue nor an approved accounting treatment. Obtain an accounting policy before commercial launch.

Sales allocation is a hypothetical mix: 20% boosters, 15% singles, 25% collections, 30% cosmetics, 10% expansions; it partitions the same gross total rather than adding sales for currency purchases and downstream items. No expansion product is implicitly approved. The downloadable model includes all six categories and conversion 2% / 5% / 10% crossed with $5 / $15 / $30 spending sensitivity. Future mobile mode substitutes a configurable 30% platform-rate assumption and zero web fixed processing fee. Browser and mobile fees are separate scenarios, not automatically added together. Regional platform terms and taxes require specific review.

## Observations and launch prerequisites

The administrator report separates ordinary/sandbox cohorts, provider test amounts, wallet origins and current outstanding balances. Selected date windows apply to historical purchases, payments and activity; outstanding units, reveal completion and current collections are explicitly current snapshots. Historical product/currency/delivery/version terms come from immutable receipts. Exports contain aggregates rather than player identities, emails, card data or credentials. Test gross amounts remain sandbox observations; actual sales, cash receipts and recognized revenue remain zero. No current retention, conversion or profitability measurement is claimed.

Before any real commercial launch, obtain owner-approved pricing/content, verified asset rights, merchant/payment approval for the exact catalog and trading/randomized model, consumer disclosures, purchase/refund/dispute terms, age-appropriate controls, privacy/data-retention policy and jurisdiction-specific tax registration/remittance advice. Technical tests establish none of those legal conclusions. Stripe lists restricted business categories and requires review where applicable: [Stripe restricted businesses](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses). Randomized-product odds and payment requirements need platform review: [Apple review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) and [Google Play payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en). Terms and regional exceptions may change. No mobile billing or real-money activation is authorized here.

Genuine provider tests use [Stripe test-mode procedures](https://docs.stripe.com/testing), not real cards or money. Live keys and live provider objects remain rejected. A permanently sandbox account and sandbox-origin test Aether may exercise a premium random product; this is an isolated testing exception. Ordinary commercially purchased or refunded currency cannot fund the restricted randomized route. Native PostgreSQL regressions preserve that boundary.

Reporting window clarification: provider financial totals are the latest verified state of orders **created in the selected window**, including their effective refunds/disputes. They are not a cashflow report grouped by refund event date. Existing financial investigation/history tools retain the underlying individual events.

Scenario interpretation: the profile-level 25% optional-cosmetic budget and business-level 30% cosmetic sales allocation are independent sensitivity inputs. Profile rows are alternative player scenarios, not a weighted population forecast. Align these inputs and provide explicit cohort weights before treating them as one population projection; the current output makes no such empirical claim.
