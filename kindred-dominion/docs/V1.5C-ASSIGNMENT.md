# Kindred Dominion — V1.5C
## Core Economy, Currencies, Player Progression and Rewards

Repository: `Revty79/kindereddominion`

You are responsible for implementing V1.5C of Kindred Dominion according to the following approved design direction.

Kindred Dominion is a commercially oriented, standalone collectible tactical card game. We are building a sustainable game economy that will eventually support browser, Android and iOS releases, real-money purchases, player trading and expandable collectible content.

This assignment concerns the core economy infrastructure and gameplay progression. Do not implement live commercial payments or the full card shop during this milestone.

## 1. Prerequisites and implementation safeguards

Before modifying the application:

- Inspect the current repository, database schema, migration history, architecture, deployment configuration and existing project documentation.
- Verify the status of V1.5A/B and the separately assigned gameplay viewport corrections.
- Confirm that prerequisite milestones have been accepted by the owner. If acceptance is outstanding, limit work to non-mutating inspection and implementation planning, then report the blocker.
- Establish a recoverable baseline.
- Preserve existing accounts, collections, custom decks, matches, player statistics, administrator permissions and artwork.
- Do not reset or reseed existing player data.
- Follow the established development, testing, backup and deployment workflows.
- Perform implementation and destructive testing only in an isolated development or test environment.
- Do not automatically deploy database changes to production.

The existing game mechanics remain authoritative. Do not modify established placement rules, scoring, evolution, abilities, deck limits or match rules unless specifically required to correct an independently verified defect. Report any such defect separately.

## 2. Approved economy principles

The following principles govern this implementation and future milestones:

- The founding collection consists of 150 permanent gameplay card identities.
- Nature, Mythical, Arcane, Undead and Constructs contain 30 founding cards each.
- New players retain their existing choice of starting Kindred.
- New players receive the 30-card founding collection for their selected Kindred and its recommended 15-card starter deck.
- Every founding gameplay card must remain obtainable through free gameplay progression.
- Ordinary free acquisition generally grants the first copy of a card.
- Additional copies may be obtained through premium purchases, tournaments, advanced achievements, mastery rewards and future player trading.
- Card ownership is quantity-based.
- Owning one copy permits one copy in an individual deck, subject to existing deck rules.
- The same owned copy may be referenced by multiple saved decks.
- Progression levels do not directly increase gameplay card statistics or combat power.
- Rarity must not inherently determine gameplay strength.
- Cosmetics will be separate from gameplay ownership.
- All economy mutations must be server-authoritative, transactional and auditable.
- Routine economy configuration must not require source-code changes.

## 3. Currency infrastructure

Implement a reusable, extensible currency system with two initial currencies.

### Gold

Gold is the standard earnable currency.

Gold can be earned through eligible gameplay, challenges, achievements, introductory progression and authorized promotional rewards.

Gold will eventually purchase ordinary boosters and first-copy cards.

### Aether

Aether is the premium currency.

Aether will eventually be purchasable using real money and may also be awarded through exceptional achievements, tournaments and authorized promotions.

During V1.5C and V1.5D, no live commercial purchases are permitted.

Administrator-issued testing currency must be distinguishable from legitimately earned or commercially acquired currency.

### Currency requirements

Implement:

- Persistent player wallets.
- Integer-based currency accounting.
- Unique currency identifiers.
- Configurable display names and descriptions.
- Currency activation controls.
- Immutable transaction ledger.
- Transaction types and reason codes.
- Transaction timestamps.
- Associated account, match, reward, purchase or grant references.
- Idempotency keys.
- Transactional balance updates.
- Prevention of negative available balances.
- Authorized compensating transactions for corrections.
- Economy configuration version references.
- Reconciliation between balances and transaction history.

Do not directly overwrite balances as an ordinary administrative operation.

Currency adjustments must generate auditable ledger entries.

Distinguish currency origin, including earned, promotional, administrator-granted, sandbox and future purchased currency, without requiring a separate visible wallet for every origin.

No currency cash-out, player currency transfers or real-money purchase processing is authorized.

## 5. Match rewards

Implement configurable rewards for eligible completed matches.

Initial proposed reward table:

| Match type | Victory | Draw | Loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy computer | 15 Gold | 8 Gold | 5 Gold |
| Normal computer | 25 Gold | 12 Gold | 8 Gold |
| Hard computer | 40 Gold | 20 Gold | 12 Gold |
| Expert computer | 60 Gold | 30 Gold | 18 Gold |
| Eligible multiplayer | 50 Gold | 25 Gold | 15 Gold |
| Sandbox or practice | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Map these proposed difficulty categories to the application's actual implemented difficulty levels.

Do not invent unsupported computer difficulty modes merely to populate the table.

Victory, draw and legitimate loss rewards are required.

Reward processing must use authoritative persisted match outcomes rather than client-submitted results.

Provide:

- Idempotent match settlement.
- Match completion validation.
- Reward eligibility determination.
- Reward-rule version capture.
- Persistent reward records.
- Server-side currency and XP grants.
- Player-facing post-match reward summary.
- Administrative visibility into granted, denied and pending rewards.

A restarted application, repeated request, interrupted connection or duplicate match settlement must not create duplicate rewards.

Separate valid gameplay forfeits from abusive or immediately abandoned matches.

## 6. Exploitation prevention

Design a configurable anti-farming framework.

Consider:

- Extremely short matches.
- Repeated immediate surrender.
- Inactive participants.
- Self-play using multiple accounts.
- Repeated arranged victories.
- Repeated matches between the same accounts.
- Automated reward farming.
- Replayed settlement requests.
- Invalid or manipulated client-side match outcomes.
- Rapid account creation for introductory rewards.

Use proportionate safeguards, server-side evidence and auditable eligibility decisions.

Do not permanently punish players solely because they repeatedly play with friends or because they lose frequently.

Separate prevention, detection, administrative review and disciplinary actions.

Provide configurable reward cooldowns, limits and diminishing-return policies where appropriate.

Avoid hardcoded limits that cannot be adjusted after observing legitimate gameplay.

- Configurable thresholds.
- Milestone rewards.
- Progress indicators.
- Achievement integration.
