# V1.4 normal player battles

Log in, choose a starter or save your own legal deck, then open **Play**.

1. Choose the saved **Battle deck**. Drafts, illegal decks and unrestricted development decks are excluded from ordinary selection.
2. Select **Classic** or **Domination** and one of the available **Computer** formations. These are the existing published starter/mixed decks using the existing tactical computer engine. The server filters them through the saved deck's actual format/Kindred rules; no new AI difficulty or gameplay rule was introduced.
3. Review your formation, 15 owned cards, format, Kindred restriction, battlefield dimensions, opening-hand count and scoring description, then **Begin match**.
4. Review the five full-bleed opening cards. Keep all or select cards to exchange once. Selection/exchange indicators remain outside the permanent face.
5. Select a hand card, choose a highlighted legal tile and review the immediate consequences. **Confirm placement** commits the action and lets the computer respond. Hidden draws resolve after confirmation; the preview does not claim to show the computer's future response.
6. **Current** power appears beneath the battlefield card; the face retains printed power. Positive/negative changes, ownership, tokens, fortification, protection and selection remain gameplay context. Influence diagrams face right for Jade and mirror horizontally for Amber under the existing rules. Artwork and text remain upright and unchanged.
7. Use **Inspect** on hand/field cards for full size and current power layers. Row sidebars distinguish power from points; Domination also shows surviving-creature column context. Passing, forced passing, surrender and all endgame rules use the same server engine.
8. At a legitimate conclusion, inspect the saved result and return to the main menu. Profile/history derive outcomes from once-per-match verified completion rows. Watch the public replay from **Match history**, including its final position.

## Resume and recovery

Committed actions save automatically. After leaving or refreshing, open **Play → Continue your campaign** or **Match history → Resume match**. The same match ID, revision, hand, battlefield, opponent and historical content are loaded from PostgreSQL. A new browser can retrieve them after login. Browser storage is not authoritative.

If another session acted first, stale actions are rejected. Use **Reload saved match** in the error message, then select a card again. The application does not overwrite the server state or silently start a replacement match. Reviewing recorded resolution temporarily disables ordinary placements until returning to the current battlefield.

## Server boundaries and validation

The backend accepts an owned deck ID, scoring choice and optional published opponent ID. It revalidates ownership, deck legality, format, opponent availability and every action through the shared engine. Seeds and starting player remain server-chosen. No client-provided result, reward, private opponent hand/draw, RNG state or replay signature becomes trusted input/output. Public responses expose only permitted information; added owner/facing and computer-label fields are public context, not hidden state.

No migration or engine/balance/card-definition change was required for this checkpoint. A selected computer formation is retained through the existing immutable deck/content snapshot; historic matches remain reconstructable. New tests verify published-only/format-specific selection, foreign rejection, a complete selected Domination game, replay integrity, once-only statistics and API restart persistence. The real browser also exchanges an opening card, resumes after refresh, completes the selected match and reviews its replay.
