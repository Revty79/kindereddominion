## Architecture and preservation

The existing authoritative inventory, currency and acquisition services remain central. Ownership lots retain original source/edition/environment and later transfer ancestry. Lifetime first-copy records never reset after giving a card away. Reservations reduce available copies without changing ownership. Both participants' player/source locks are ordered; acceptance checks the exact current immutable version, current configuration, blocks/restrictions, both sides' eligibility and precise reserved lots. It records both outgoing/incoming acquisitions, transfers, a permanent receipt and final state in the same PostgreSQL transaction. There is no committed accepted-but-unsettled intermediate state. Public responses expose the viewer's relevant ownership; private quantities, drafts, reports and account data are not disclosed to counterparties.

The new E.6 worker processes at most fifty due offers per batch, runs at startup and every thirty seconds when the server economy is enabled, avoids overlapping batches, and awaits its in-flight work at shutdown. Expiry closes/releases a known offer transactionally, then projects persistent notifications separately. Failures retry without requiring either browser to be open. Event records remain the durable notification outbox. Real PostgreSQL tests verify delivery to both parties without inbox reads, worker restart/deduplication and recovery after an observed database statement timeout. Application test pools remain explicitly managed by the integration harness.

## Economy impact and provisional recommendations

`scripts/trading-model.ts` and `scripts/trading-simulation.mts` produce `V1.5E-SIMULATION.json`. These are deterministic planning simulations, not live activity or revenue. They combine the existing C model with a separate bounded card-supply/liquidity stress model. Every modeled trade conserves source quantities and generates zero Gold/XP. A 100-player, twelve-week balanced cohort clears182 reciprocal trades; a90/5/3/1/1 Kindred population clears24 under the same1300 legitimately earned extra-copy supply. Ineligible farming generates no copies or trades. These figures depend on disclosed matching/demand assumptions and do not forecast player behavior.

The present free additional-copy proposal supplies six identities: weekly `N013`, `M014`, `A014`, `U014`, `C014`, plus the deterministic one-time Veteran Nature reward `N001`. Mastery presently grants Gold, not additional cards. Casual16-match weeks miss the20-match weekly source and first reach Veteran in week7; one commonly available source alone does not make a diverse reciprocal market. Recommend owner review of varied/rotating free additional-copy pools through existing configuration before treating the provisional market as mature. This report does not change approved pricing, permanent policy or gameplay.

A player still begins with30 cards and needs at most24 guaranteed-new five-card packs for the remaining120. The optional proposed tutorial gift is one of those24, leaving23 paid packs/5750Gold. Legitimate rewards/trades may lower the remaining pack need; they never justify increasing it. Current C/D reference scenarios complete first-copy collection in roughly10–15.7active hours across6–32calendar days, assuming the modeled objectives/claims. Base-match Gold alone yields about128–203Gold/hour and74–117minutes per250Gold booster; it does not satisfy an earlier provisional30–60minute base-income target. Objective contributions and engagement assumptions are shown explicitly rather than silently increasing completion time. Extra playable copies are a separate acquisition goal. Trading cannot mint currency, card quantities or combat bonuses.
