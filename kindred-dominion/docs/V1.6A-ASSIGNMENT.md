## 2. New starting collection structure

Each new player will continue selecting one founding Kindred.

The starting collection will contain:

- All 30 unique founding cards from the selected Kindred.
- Ten deliberately selected additional playable copies.
- Forty total playable card copies.
- A recommended 15-card starter battle deck.

The 40-card collection is not a 40-card battle deck.

Preserve the existing 15-card standard battle deck size and existing card-specific and game-mode copy limits.

Make starter collection definitions configurable and versioned through the existing administration architecture.

## 4. Select additional starting copies

For each Kindred, propose ten additional playable copies.

Favor useful foundational cards, accessible placement requirements and cards that support the Kindred's primary tactical identity.

The objective is to allow meaningful duplicate-based strategies without excessively strengthening starting collections.

Additional copies do not necessarily need to represent ten different card identities if a different distribution is demonstrably better and permitted by existing rules.

Do not modify card gameplay statistics or existing copy restrictions to make the proposed collection work.

For each proposed additional copy, document:

- Permanent card ID.
- Creature name.
- Existing placement requirement.
- Existing power.
- Existing ability or tactical role.
- Reason for including an additional copy.
- Potential balance concerns.

Provide all five proposed 40-copy starting collections for owner review.

## 5. Recommended starter battle decks

Construct a recommended 15-card battle deck for each Kindred using its proposed starting collection.

Each deck must:

- Be legal under existing gameplay rules.
- Respect actual owned quantities.
- Include sufficient accessible opening cards.
- Have a meaningful tactical identity.
- Provide reasonable placement development.
- Include usable higher-requirement cards.
- Support relevant evolution strategies.
- Be suitable for inexperienced players.
- Demonstrate meaningful use of additional starting copies where appropriate.

Avoid creating starter decks that frequently become unplayable because of their opening hands.

Where possible, demonstrate at least two alternative legal deck strategies using each starting collection.
