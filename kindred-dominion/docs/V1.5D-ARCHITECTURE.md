## Shared services and invariants

- Reuse C's configuration drafts, immutable versions/publication, audited rollback, player locks, currency lots/consumption, acquisition ledger, ownership lots and lifetime first-copy history.
- Add an asynchronous publication validator/identity hook to the existing configuration service for shop references. Do not create a second configuration system.
- Shop configuration separates Kindred, collection membership, rarity, acquisition eligibility, product contents and deck format. Rarity metadata in the permanent founding publication remains unchanged; the current owner catalog is still Unassigned.
- Store stable product/collection/cosmetic identities separately from immutable configuration revisions. Cosmetic IDs retain their gameplay-card relationship; product retirement does not remove ownership.
- A server quote captures product, shop/economy/content versions, exact price, eligible count and disclosed completion/odds policy. Purchase confirmation rechecks current availability, configuration, lifetime and current ownership, limits and exact quote terms under the player lock. Stale terms require another confirmation; clients never submit prices/results.
- Currency deduction, inventory/cosmetic acquisition, lifetime records, purchase receipt and pack result commit atomically. Idempotency replays the original receipt. Insufficient funds or late asset failure rolls everything back.
- A shop pack's results are generated with crypto randomness and granted during settlement. Reveal is presentation only, with persistent progress and recoverable results; replaying animation never grants more cards. Existing local testing sampler behavior stays separate.
- Actual production purchases have no seeded random option. Seeded sampling exists only in pure simulations/tests or an explicitly isolated administrator preview.

## Product behavior

The initial DEV proposal includes ordinary/targeted first-copy boosters at the C reference prices, premium additional-copy boosters, direct first-copy/additional-copy cards, complete Kindred/all-founding bundles and reusable expansion/cosmetic products. Configuration is not selected by migration or automatically activated on hosted TEST.

Each ordinary first-copy selection excludes both lifetime-acquired and currently owned identities, then removes each selected card from the pack pool. Slot rarity weights renormalize over available permitted rarities. A strict guaranteed slot never silently substitutes another rarity; exhaustion is disclosed and may make that product unavailable. Ordinary proposal slots disclose their conditional fallback when a required rarity pool is depleted. Final packs are reduced and prorated explicitly before confirmation. Zero eligible cards disables the product.

Proposed rarity counts remain 12/8/5/3/2 per Kindred. Explicit assignments must be reviewed against actual cards, including low-power tactical cards and accessible evolution prerequisites; power alone does not determine rarity. Publish the complete proposed roster and rationale for owner review, separate from permanent commercial approval.

Gold bundles grant eligible missing first copies; additional bundles explicitly grant another copy of every listed card. Gold completion prices should be compared against guaranteed-new booster equivalents, rather than accidentally charging several times more for the same deterministic completion. Record provisional discounts and the differences between Aether single-card prices and the supplied cash modeling targets. None is an approved permanent commercial policy.

## Cosmetics and formats

Cosmetic ownership is independent of playable quantities. Prioritize alternate-art equipment with immutable approved asset references and the existing asset importer/manifest. Future card backs/borders/animated artwork/titles/effects are modeled but inactive until rendered. Tests use existing temporary illustration assets, not new commercial art or changes to the founding illustrations.

Collection/deck/hand/battlefield/inspection continue to use the existing artwork-first portrait component. Presentation overrides reference the same permanent card ID; they do not change statistics or engine definitions. Freeze cosmetic and rarity presentation with a newly started match; purchases/equipment/configuration changes do not rewrite saved match snapshots. Archived ownership retains its approved historical artwork reference.

Formats are reusable published deck-eligibility definitions: Founders, Standard, Unlimited and inactive event-specific Draft/Special examples. Validate the selected deck against allowed/excluded collections, Kindreds, rarities, IDs, size and quantity limits, in addition to existing ownership/gameplay validation. Do not inspect the player's unrelated premium collection or create a tournament/entry-fee engine. Existing decks use the base rules when no shop format is published.

## Provenance and recovery

First-copy, starter, unknown legacy, administrator and sandbox copies remain bound. Additional achievement/mastery copies can supply the later free trading path. Purchased additional copies may carry configurable holding periods. Spending administrator/sandbox currency must not launder test assets into ordinary tradeable purchased copies. Sandbox accounts, currency and resulting assets stay separate; actual transfers remain inactive until E.

Compensation is an explicit audited transaction against the original purchase's unspent/unreserved acquisition lots and original consumed currency origins. Never debit unrelated copies or credits. Preserve source links for later F refunds/disputes. Provider-independent order/receipt references are prepared without activating checkout.
