# V1.4 collections, custom decks and testing acquisition

## Your collection

Register, open **My Collection**, inspect the five Founding Kindreds and confirm one starter. The existing approved grant remains unchanged: one copy of the chosen Kindred's 30 founding cards plus its exact 15-card recommended deck. It is a permanent, one-time choice, recorded atomically in PostgreSQL. New ordinary accounts do not receive all 150 cards.

**Card Catalog** browses all published founding cards independently of ownership. **My Collection** defaults to owned cards and shows quantities. Ownership filters also reveal cards not yet owned. Search by permanent number, name or ability; filter Kindred, placement type/rank, classification, mechanics, set and power, and sort by collectible number, name or power. Inspect uses the same full-bleed renderer at readable size. Acquisition history shows actual ledger transactions, including starter grants and explicitly designated testing acquisitions.

Multiple copies are collection quantities; a card may be used in multiple saved decks. Owned quantities do not override deck copy limits. No browser-local field can grant ownership.

## Custom decks and drafts

1. Open **Deck Builder → Create deck** and choose a name, scoring, format and Kindred restriction.
2. Add owned cards from the visual card browser; remove them or adjust quantities in the selected-card strip. **Use my recommended starter** is an optional starting point, not a restriction on custom construction.
3. **Save draft** retains incomplete work. Drafts may have incomplete or otherwise illegal composition, but must still reference available published cards and owned quantities. They cannot be selected as the ordinary default or used in a match.
4. Resolve the live legality messages, then **Save deck**. Playing decks require the existing approved 15-card, copy, format and Kindred rules; the backend checks them again before play.
5. **Select for battle** persists the preference. Edit/rename, duplicate and delete are available. Deletion keeps historical matches intact. Optimistic revisions reject stale edits. Converting a selected deck back to a draft clears its battle preference.

The backend reuses the shared engine validators. Saving a draft does not weaken the playing rules, create cards or bypass ownership. Unrestricted development decks remain separately marked and excluded from the normal player battle selector.

## Explicit temporary local-testing sampler

No production pack economy, prices, rarity probabilities, currency rewards or purchases have been approved or added. To demonstrate real acquisition and pack opening, V1.4 provides a bounded **local-testing-only** policy:

- Disabled by default; only DEV and guarded integration environments may enable it.
- Requires at least one completed, verified normal computer match. Sandbox/development matches do not qualify.
- One entitlement per account, independent of request identifiers, refresh, logout/login and concurrent retries.
- Default contents: **A001, M001, C001**, one copy each, with no random rarity roll. These are configurable published IDs, not permanent economy decisions.
- Cards enter inventory only when the player opens the pack. Opening and all quantities commit atomically with an acquisition ledger entry. Duplicate cards increase quantity.
- Reopening the result reviews the same cards without granting more copies. The immutable published content version/artwork is retained for historical review.
- No experience, level, currency or competitive reward counter is invented or updated.

Enable only in the private DEV configuration, then restart the backend:

```dotenv
LOCAL_TEST_ACQUISITION=true
LOCAL_TEST_PACK_CARDS=a001,m001,c001
```

After a normal battle, open **Card Packs → Claim one testing sampler → Open sampler**, then **View My Collection**. UI and transaction history explicitly identify the test policy. Configuration accepts one to ten published card IDs; repeated IDs become multiple copies. The policy's permanent one-per-account marker remains `local-sampler-v1` even if its configured contents change. Hosted configuration refuses enabled testing acquisition.

Setting the flag false stops new claims/openings; already acquired cards and historical receipts remain intact. Opened receipts remain reviewable. Future approved acquisition policies can use the same ownership ledger and entitlement boundary without replacing player accounts or collections.

## Storage and safety

Migration `1795726000000_decks_and_testing_packs.cjs` adds `deck.is_draft` (existing decks remain ready) and `card_pack` entitlements with owner/content/acquisition relationships and one-time uniqueness. Earlier migrations and all existing records are preserved. No card definitions, permanent IDs, image files, engine rules or balances were changed.

All collection/deck/pack routes derive the actor from the server session. Foreign IDs are rejected, client-supplied contents/grants are unsupported, illegal or unowned playing decks are rejected, and concurrent opening grants only once. Original artwork and optimized revisions remain in Git under the existing publication/import pipeline.
