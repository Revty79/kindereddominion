## Quantities, completion and provenance

`quantity` remains the default grant per identity; `quantities` optionally overrides it for guaranteed bundles. `mode: completion` grants `max(0, target − owned)` per identity. Aggregate ownership includes bound starters, all owned editions and reserved copies. This premium mode does not rewrite or reset lifetime first-copy history. Ordinary Gold products still require both no current copy and no lifetime acquisition, including after a trade or compensation.

Fixed completion price is the full product price multiplied by missing target copies / total target copies, rounded up to whole currency units. A published discount is then applied with integer ceiling. The price cannot exceed the equivalently discounted full set. Per-copy and rarity pricing multiply the actual grant quantities. Quotes include owned counts, target counts, exact grants, full/missing totals, currency, price, policy warnings and immutable configuration versions. Settlement locks the player, re-evaluates quantities and compares the full completion plan. If another acquisition changes that plan, the second confirmation is rejected without a debit. Retrying the same committed request returns the saved receipt.

Examples:

- Thirty Nature starter cards toward Nature standard: no deficits and no purchase.
- Thirty Nature starter cards toward the proposed Nature deluxe: ten missing extra copies; `ceil(1300 × 10 / 40) = 325 Aether`.
- A card with target two and ownership one grants one; ownership two or more grants zero.
- Thirty unique starter cards toward all standard: 120 missing copies, 2,400 Aether.
- Thirty unique starter cards toward all deluxe: 170 missing copies, 3,400 Aether, **only in isolated DEV until approved**.

An additional set always grants the complete configured quantities, even with a complete collection. New lots retain their source purchase and funding links. Existing bound starter lots are never relabelled. Non-sandbox paid acquisitions follow the existing purchased-fund hold; administrator-funded assets and sandbox assets remain bound. Refund and compensation use the exact original receipt quantities/lots, not substitute player cards. Consumed, reserved, disputed or transferred assets require the existing investigation/recovery process; they are not minted again or silently confiscated.

## Probabilities and randomized funding policy

Each row totals 100%; these are configured base weights. The product inspector shows both base and current conditional weights and eligible rarity counts. First-copy draws remove earlier selections and reserve cards required by later strict slots. `available_pool` explicitly renormalizes/falls back when weighted pools are depleted. A reduced first-copy pack truncates slots and prorates its price (for example, two targeted copies cost 120 Gold). Zero eligible cards disables purchase.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | 75, 20, 4, 0.9, 0.1% | 40, 30, 20, 8, 2% |
| 2 | 75, 20, 4, 0.9, 0.1% | 20, 35, 30, 12, 3% |
| 3 | 0, 80, 15, 4, 1% | 0, 50, 32, 14, 4% |
| 4 | 0, 0, 85, 12, 3% | 0, 0, 65, 25, 10% |
| 5 | 45, 30, 17, 6, 2% | 10, 20, 40, 22, 8% |

A zero Common/Uncommon base weight is not an unconditional rarity guarantee under disclosed `available_pool` fallback. Collector increases Epic/Legendary probability in every slot; slot four already has 100% Rare-or-better in the ordinary pack. Higher rarity does not mean stronger combat statistics.

Commercially funded premium randomness is rejected server-side in the shared fulfillment service. This covers legacy premium boosters, direct commercial contexts and consumed currency lots, including arbitrarily long compensation-return ancestry with cycle prevention. If the debit would consume prohibited funding, the complete transaction rolls back before any cards, pack or purchase receipt persists. Legitimate earned/promotional/admin noncommercial funds retain their existing rules. New Dominion/Collector definitions additionally require permanent sandbox designation. Verified sandbox purchases remain isolated and bound. Live commerce remains disabled; no actual money is processed.

Deluxe definitions contain `starterRecipe: {version, digest, kindreds}`. Unapproved recipes can only be active as `testOnly` products in isolated `dev`/`integration`, available solely to designated sandbox accounts. Hosted TEST requires the exact selected starter recipe's owner approval; production remains refused. Changing a recipe requires a new version/digest and reviewed product targets. **Do not mark the provisional V1.6A copy proposal approved on the owner's behalf.**

Genuine Stripe acceptance remains the existing owner-controlled sandbox workflow in [V1.5 owner acceptance](V1.5-OWNER-ACCEPTANCE.md). This milestone uses deterministic provider simulations; it does not create live products, contact homeserver, modify hosted TEST data, use real payment methods or claim genuine Stripe verification.
