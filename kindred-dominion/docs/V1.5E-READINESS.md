## Existing architecture and concrete findings

Permanent gameplay identity, published definitions, edition identities, quantities, immutable acquisition/entry ledgers, lifetime acquisition history, origin/environment-labelled ownership lots, source-lot ancestry, restrictions, purchased holding timestamps and a reserved counter already exist. D adds versioned products/rarities/collections, exact quote settlement, current/historical cosmetics, eligibility definitions and original-source compensation. Reuse them. Do not substitute artwork manifests for executable cards, add a second ownership balance, or edit historical acquisitions.

Unknown legacy ownership is explicitly bound. Starter, ordinary first-copy, administrator and sandbox assets are bound. A positive wallet purchase can grant explicit additional copies, but administrator funding cannot launder them into tradeable commercial copies. Cosmetics are separate and not tradeable. C's Veteran achievement already supplies a legitimate free additional copy after 100 eligible matches; the configuration service can define additional-copy challenge/mastery rewards. The initial proposal needs broader meaningful free supply rather than an effective payment gate.

Confirmed code finding to correct in E.1: challenge claim processing currently supplies `first_copy` provenance for every nonpermanent objective, including a configured additional-copy weekly reward. Derive first-copy versus additional-copy provenance from the individual reward definition; keep administrator/sandbox overrides and existing historical rows intact. The reserved counter alone lacks durable reservation identities, purpose/offer linkage, expiry and release history. Existing saved-deck legality already checks quantities on reads and match entry; trades must preserve deck contents and let this authoritative validation explain missing quantities. Active matches already retain deck/content/state and appearance snapshots and must remain independent.

## Reservation and provenance design

E.1 introduces immutable reservation groups and per-lot reservation terms, purpose/reference, quantities, creation/expiry and monotone release state. Creation/retry is idempotent for an exact purpose reference. Ended reservations cannot be resurrected. Lock the player, expire eligible stale reservations, then lock candidate lots in deterministic order. Reserve only available owned copies whose explicit lot flag, original provenance, environment, holding period, current identity and account restrictions allow trading. Reserved copies remain owned and playable; incompatible spending/transfers/compensation cannot consume them. Counters must reconcile to active reservation rows and never exceed remaining quantities.

Reservation APIs are internal services, not an unattached public inventory-lock endpoint. A concrete exact offer version supplies their scope. Public listings are nonbinding interest and do not reserve inventory. Expiration is enforced at action time and through bounded recovery sweeps; no browser timer is authoritative. Durable records survive application/database restart. Release never deletes ownership or history.

Incoming trade lots retain source-lot ancestry and original commercial/reward references. Traceable source provenance remains distinct from the transfer event. An incoming transfer cannot wash a bound, restricted, sandbox or held source into eligibility, reset lifetime acquisition, manufacture a new first-copy reward, or erase a payment link. Validate ancestry conservatively and bound traversal. New ownership and consumption entries preserve accounting; net edition quantities across both parties must remain identical.

## Offer lifecycle and settlement

E.2 uses an offer head, immutable exact content versions, immutable events and immutable settlement/transfer receipts. Local/UI drafts are nonbinding; submitted offers are pending. Submission is the author's explicit consent to that exact version and reserves the author's offered side. The named recipient explicitly accepts that exact version or creates a new immutable counteroffer version, reversing the consenting author/recipient and reserving the new author side. Release superseded reservations atomically. A previous confirmation never applies to changed contents. Decline, cancellation, expiration and invalidation are explicit terminal events; accepted and settled events occur together in one successful settlement transaction.

Require both sides to contribute bounded positive card quantities. No equal-rarity or monetary-equivalence restriction is imposed. There are no currency transfers, cash, cosmetics, auctions, wagers, chat or external arrangements.

Read participants, acquire both player locks in sorted identity order, then lock the offer and current trading selection. Every lifecycle operation uses the same ordering. Recheck exact version, state, server expiry, both accounts, blocks/restrictions, configured limits, identities, original lots and author reservations. Reserve/revalidate the recipient side under those locks, consume the two specific reserved sides, create both incoming acquisition lots and immutable transfer/settlement events, release reservations and finish the offer in the same transaction. Failure rolls back both sides. A same-key/exact-version retry returns the existing receipt; conflicting/stale keys cannot settle again.

Global suspension serializes through the configuration selection lock and prevents new settlements after publication. Preserve pending contents/history while suspended; expiry/cancellation/release remain available. Account restrictions lock the account and are rechecked during settlement. Sweeps must use the same deterministic participant ordering and bounded batches. Do not hold an account lock and then acquire a lower-ordered counterparty lock in an administrator shortcut.

Persist trade events as part of settlement. Notification delivery is a separately recoverable postcommit projection with unique player/event identity; failure to project cannot undo a committed trade. Fetch/recovery can fill missing notifications idempotently. No email/SMS/push provider is added.

## Threat model and proposed safeguards

| Threat | Existing or planned safeguard | Practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disposable starter/first-copy farming | Bound source lots; permanent lifetime-first-copy exclusion; deny source laundering | Does not claim bot detection or identity proof |
| Reward farming | Existing server replay verification, actual participation, cooldown/daily caps and configured advanced challenge thresholds | Automated legitimate-looking gameplay remains a review risk; no speculative blanket punishment |
| Double commitment/acceptance | Durable reservations, deterministic player locks, exact immutable versions and unique settlement receipts | Must be demonstrated under real PG18 races |
| Refunded/disputed purchase transfers | Source ancestry, original purchased holds, restrictions checked under locks; later F investigation integration | Do not confiscate unrelated cards or innocent recipients' assets |
| Offer spam/harassment | Bounded rates/outstanding offers/listings, blocks, structured reports and targeted audited restrictions | Shared networks and subjective trade imbalance alone are not guilt |
| Forged ownership/price/currency | Strict validated card-only requests and server authoritative lots/identity/rules | No currency field or client-side grant path |
| Suspicious account interactions | Private review indicators with supporting history | Indicators are not automatic bans or invasive identity requirements |
| Sandbox contamination | Permanent sandbox account flag, environment and every source-lot boundary | Sandbox/admin cards never enter ordinary Trading Hall settlement |

Initial settings are DEV proposals, not approved permanent restrictions: trading paused until explicitly published active; minimum age one hour, account level two and three legitimate completed matches; ten open offers and ten listings; ten new offers/hour and twenty settlements/day; bounded multi-card size and 24-hour default expiry with configurable maximum. Purchased origin keeps the existing proposed 168-hour hold; achievement/mastery additional copies need no added payment gate. Consider configurable twenty-match weekly Kindred challenges that grant one explicit tradeable additional copy per Kindred, without changing combat rules or fabricating past rewards. Simulate supply and legitimate participation before final recommendations. Do not activate tournaments or invent a new commercial policy to provide free copies.

## Administration, recovery and commercial compatibility

E.5 reuses versioned draft/validate/publish/history/rollback for activation, provenance policies, original holding periods, account/size/rate/expiry limits and emergency suspension. Restrict individual accounts with an explicit reason/audit, inspect pending and settled offers, private reports, source ancestry and reservations, invalidate unsettled offers and release stale reservations. Never rewrite settled history. Safe corrective tooling must inspect original unconsumed downstream lots and dependencies; when a complete reversal is unsafe, present an investigation receipt and require explicit bounded compensation rather than uncontrolled inventory editing.

F can associate original purchased currency/card grants with provider-independent orders/events. Trace transfer ancestry for disputes, serialize new restrictions against settlement, and distinguish a reversible original grant from assets transferred to another legitimate player. Existing D compensation must continue refusing reserved or consumed original lots. No commercial API, real payment or hosted mutation belongs in E.

Permanent card identity, digital acquisition lot, cosmetic identity and a possible future physical serial are distinct. A future physical redemption provider may link its authenticated serial/event to an auditable acquisition. An ordinary NFC tag identifier is not proof of authenticity/ownership. No serial registry, NFC/hardware dependency, redemption or manufacturing system is implemented now.
