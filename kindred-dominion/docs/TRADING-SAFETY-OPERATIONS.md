# Trading safety and recovery

Ordinary players use Trading Hall → Trading safety. A block prevents both new offers and settlement between the two players. Existing offers retain their histories and reservations: decline or cancel them, let them expire, or request an administrator investigation. Unblocking does not submit or accept an offer. Reports require a submitted trade and its exact current version, a structured category and a private explanation. Reports are allegations; they do not automatically punish another player or transfer assets. Administrators alone can inspect evidence and append private investigation reviews. Limits for reports and block lists are versioned trading configuration.

Administrators use protected Administration → Trading Hall. Create a private draft from current settings, or explicitly select the latest provisional DEV proposal. Review/edit the activity flag, eligibility, limits, holds and origin allowlists, validate/save, then publish with a reason. Immutable configuration history supports controlled rollback. Draft/proposal creation never activates settings or rewrites already published versions. A pause serializes against in-flight settlements: a transaction that already owns the current policy lock finishes before publication; subsequent offers/settlements are rejected. Pending reservations remain recoverable through closure. Production activation remains disabled.

For a reported transaction, inspect its exact offer ID. Retain its versions, events, report evidence, settlement/transfer/acquisition references, original lot ancestry and relevant audit references. Repeat pair activity is a review signal, not proof of abuse. Never infer fraud solely from rarity, price, reciprocal trading or a report.

To stop an unsettled offer, load its current exact version, enter a reason and invalidate it. This closes only an open offer and atomically releases its reservations. The reason and report evidence remain in privileged audit records; participants receive a generic invalidation event/notification. Settled transactions cannot be invalidated or silently reversed.

To restrict a participant, enter their permanent player ID, a reason and optional future UTC end time. A restriction prevents subsequent offers/settlements while preserving their ownership and ability to close offers. Remove it through another audited operation when appropriate. To restrict a suspicious acquisition through downstream transfers, identify its original root lot from ancestry and apply a source restriction. Descendant tradeability rechecks the root; quantities and innocent unrelated assets remain unchanged. Removing a restriction is also explicit and audited.

Use the expiry maintenance action to process a bounded batch of overdue offers. If an investigation proves a retained reservation belongs to an already closed known trade, load that exact offer and choose recovery of closed-offer reservations with an audit reason. Open commitments are refused. This correction changes reservation counters/records together and never grants or removes ownership. Unknown legacy reservations remain untouched. A mismatch with no verifiable offer/source requires investigation using the preserved records and a verified backup; do not reset counters or delete history.

An ambiguous response can be retried with its original operation key; conflicting terms are rejected. The interface preserves the operation key until preparing another operation. After any correction, refresh and inspect the receipt and reconciliation results. Record further compensation through explicit existing administrator grant/correction services only after establishing the legitimate source and affected quantity. Such grants retain administrator provenance and cannot launder assets into ordinary trading.

Backup/recovery uses the established operator procedure and paired source/database receipts. Restore a verified separate database before considering any recovery promotion. Existing histories and immutable source assets stay retained. Routine TEST updates remain `sudo kindred-update`; these development instructions do not authorize a hosted update or a production data change.
