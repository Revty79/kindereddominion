## Implementation and architecture

Structured Shop tools create disabled private products under fresh portable IDs; duplicate without copying live featured/promotional placement; configure stable product purpose, category, fixed pools/collections, included/excluded identities, per-card targets, pack slots/weights/depletion, active currency, direct rarity/override prices, quantity, artwork revision, availability windows, limits, featured ordering and promotions. Collection membership and draft expansion containers reuse existing published gameplay IDs; new engine content still requires separate content publication. Cosmetic fields include card/Kindred/theme and existing asset/revision approval tools. Commerce supports structured duplication and versioned model assumptions. Existing audited ordinary/admin grants and sandbox-only test purchases remain authoritative.

The standard workflow is create draft, configure contents/ownership, assign approved artwork, establish price/eligibility, validate, preview/simulate, save, publish with reason and active-version comparison, observe, revise/archive. Mathematical errors (invalid sums, contradictory financial assumptions, unsupported cosmetic rarity pricing, invalid quantities/identities) block; price/packaging/value/content-approval warnings are distinct. A missing optional product cover may use the existing labeled preview; missing required cosmetic artwork prevents activation. Previewing grants no assets, inserts no quote, changes no selection and cannot bypass publication. Account-specific proposed quotes are explicitly nonpurchasable previews.

New server modules: `shop-diagnostics`, `store-administration`, `store-model-settings`, `store-model`, `store-report`. They reuse configuration, quantity, pricing, draw, economy, provider and ownership services. New protected endpoints: `POST /api/admin/shop/analysis`, `POST /api/admin/store/model`, `GET /api/admin/store/report`. Administrator roles, CSRF/session controls, strict inputs and rate limits apply. Analysis/report database work is repeatable-read/read-only; observation exports contain aggregate data, not private player/payment identifiers. All mutations still use the established audited transactional services. No new migration or replacement ledger is introduced.

The report groups immutable product purpose/source/currency/publication snapshots, top-100 product groups plus complete totals, purchase/reversal quantities, pack reveal states, promotional confirmations/settlements/savings, provider-separated test orders/refunds/disputes/pending/failures/review, currency origins, current outstanding/held balances, current founding completion/additional copies and trades. Currency units are not cash. Provider consideration is counted once; spending previously purchased Aether is not another sale. Actual sales/cash/recognized revenue fields remain zero. Window bounds are strict and limited to 366 days; current snapshots are labeled separately from windowed observations. Promo quotes are not unique impressions or causal conversion evidence.

[Economy/profitability review](V1.6H-ECONOMY-AND-PROFITABILITY.md) and [deterministic model output](v1.6h/STORE-MODEL.json) cover 30 unique/40 playable starting copies, 120 missing, 24 actual-draw guaranteed-new packs, Gold trajectories, targeted/single/completion/additional/deluxe options, premium expected value/rarity/repeats, whole-package funding with leftovers, cash alternatives, promotions, trade supply conservation and cosmetic budgets. Finance includes 100/500/1,000/5,000/10,000/50,000 MAU, category sales mix, average Checkout, fees/refunds/disputes/costs/break-even and independent mobile assumptions. These are configurable hypotheses, never approved prices, actual profitability, market value, retention evidence or accounting/legal policy.
