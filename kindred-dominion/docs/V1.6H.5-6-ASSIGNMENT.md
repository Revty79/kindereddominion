# Kindred Dominion — V1.6H.5–6
## Cosmetic Ownership, Customization and Featured Store Inventory

Repository: `Revty79/kindereddominion`

Complete the reusable cosmetic commerce and featured-content systems.

Cosmetics are a major intended revenue source and must not alter gameplay power or competitive eligibility.

## 1. Existing implementation review

Inspect existing cosmetic definitions, alternate artwork ownership, full-bleed rendering, player preferences, Developer Studio and shop functionality.

Reuse functioning infrastructure.

Preserve immutable published creature artwork and permanent gameplay identities.

## 2. Alternate artwork

Support purchasing, earning, owning, inspecting and equipping alternate artwork.

Each artwork variant must have its own stable cosmetic identity and reference the underlying gameplay card.

Owning alternate artwork must not automatically grant another playable card copy.

Support:

- Individual alternate artwork.
- Themed artwork bundles.
- Complete evolution-chain artwork bundles.
- Kindred-wide artwork collections.
- Promotional artwork.
- Future animated artwork.

Initial commercial modeling ranges:

- Standard alternate artwork: $0.99–$1.99.
- Premium alternate artwork: $2.99–$4.99.
- Evolution-chain artwork: $4.99–$7.99.
- Themed bundles: $9.99–$19.99.
- Future animated artwork: $4.99–$9.99.

These are provisional values, not approved live prices.

## 3. Artwork Gallery

Create a browsable collection of eligible cosmetic artwork.

Allow players to:

- Inspect original and alternate appearances.
- Compare artwork.
- Filter by creature, Kindred and theme.
- Identify owned variants.
- Purchase eligible variants.
- Equip owned variants.
- Restore original artwork.

Preserve the existing full-bleed card renderer.

Gameplay information must remain readable.

Alternate artwork must not change card abilities, rarity, power, placement requirements or deck legality.

## 4. Card backs

Implement functional cosmetic card-back ownership and selection.

Support five initial standard Kindred designs:

- Nature.
- Mythical.
- Arcane.
- Undead.
- Constructs.

New players should receive their originally selected Kindred's standard card back without payment.

Provide an idempotent grant for eligible existing players once official assets and configurations are approved.

Support additional purchasable card backs.

Proposed commercial range: $0.99–$3.99.

Do not encode hidden card identity, rarity or deck information into opponent-visible card backs.

## 5. Battlefield appearances

Implement reusable personal battlefield customization.

Initial proposed appearances:

- Ancient Woodland — Nature.
- Dragon's Sanctum — Mythical.
- Astral Nexus — Arcane.
- Forgotten Crypt — Undead.
- The Great Foundry — Constructs.

These are working content names.

Give players their starting Kindred's standard battlefield appearance for free.

Support additional premium battlefield appearances.

Proposed commercial range: $2.99–$7.99.

Battlefield appearances must not alter:

- Board dimensions.
- Tile positions.
- Gameplay rules.
- Placement indicators.
- Card statistics.
- Effect previews.
- Opponent action readability.
- Score calculations.

Preserve the V1.6 gameplay viewport requirements.

Support a neutral fallback battlefield for competitive readability and accessibility.

## 6. Card treatments

Implement reusable decorative card treatment infrastructure.

Support appropriate presentation for:

- Metallic effects.
- Holographic effects.
- Decorative borders or edge treatments.
- Future animated treatments.

Prioritize lightweight rendering and readable gameplay information.

Proposed commercial range: $1.99–$4.99.

Avoid excessive GPU usage, flashing effects or animations that obscure the battlefield.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Only expose purchasable treatments that actually function.

## 8. Cosmetic ownership and preferences

Maintain separate ownership records for gameplay assets and cosmetic assets.

Store equipped cosmetic preferences persistently.

Ensure cosmetics remain owned after:

- Trading a gameplay card.
- Changing decks.
- Changing Kindred preferences.
- Application restart.
- Browser refresh.

A player who no longer owns the underlying gameplay card should retain purchased artwork ownership, but may only use it when otherwise eligible.

Apply existing refund and dispute safeguards.

Cosmetic trading remains disabled unless separately authorized.
