Security findings and boundaries:

- Anonymous, player and developer roles cannot use trading administration. Strict input schemas, server session/role lookup, same-origin/CSRF checks and owner-scoped queries remain enforced. A test initially expected an unauthenticated mutation to reach authentication; the existing CSRF guard correctly rejects a tokenless request first. The final case supplies an anonymous session to test authentication independently.
- Concurrent block/report retries record one operation. A restriction-versus-settlement race resolves to either an entirely settled exchange before restriction or a denied exchange with unchanged ownership; later offers are denied. No asset transfer depends on notification delivery.
- Starter/first-copy, unknown legacy, administrator and sandbox provenance remain excluded from ordinary trading. Immutable lifetime first-copy records survive transfers; reacquiring a traded-away identity cannot farm guaranteed-new packs. Free additional-copy rewards remain legitimate explicitly claimed gameplay sources.
- Reservations, exact source-lot consumption, immutable versions and atomic bilateral settlement prevent overselling and card duplication. Root restrictions remain effective after subsequent transfers. Configured offer/settlement/expiry limits contain reservation spam without inventing combat or rarity restrictions.
- Reports are not proof; blocks/reports do not issue grants, currency rewards, penalties or automatic ownership reversals. Social collusion and account automation remain operational risks requiring observation and owner policy; no demonstrated duplication or privilege bypass remains from this work.
- Commercial payment/refund defenses remain F work. No real payments, external messaging, physical ownership transfers or new gameplay mechanics are introduced here.
