## Design review concerns

- Compare pure-deck outliers and first-player effects in the tables above before making changes. Card statistics reflect deck composition, tactical matchups and this particular AI. They do not establish causal strength.
- Fortification protects only unoccupied territory; it does not directly raise a surviving creature’s score. The computer can undervalue long-term defensive plans. Overthrow is an intentional counter.
- Replacement and evolution can score on full boards. High-power upgrades and cheap repeated population are important review candidates, especially under Domination.
- Resurrection retains weakening and lineage limits. A creature defeated by nonpositive power may return and immediately die; this is intentional existing behavior and can make apparent resilience weaker than a card name suggests.
- Nature’s venom is immediate weakening. No unimplemented ongoing poison status is advertised.
- Larger-field, human-play and broader strategy search may produce different conclusions.

All-card coverage: 150/150 appeared in tested decks. 14 definitions had no actual placement in these runs: pack-matriarch, verdant-colossus, swarm-sovereign, ruby-drake, nova-beast, solar-archon, null-inquisitor, star-cartographer, mirror-sovereign, tomb-breaker, necropolis-heart, last-bell, night-regent, clockwork-dreadnought.
