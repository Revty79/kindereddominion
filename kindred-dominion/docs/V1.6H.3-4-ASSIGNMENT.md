## 8. Standard Kindred collections

Create five fixed-content standard collection products.

Each contains one copy of each of the corresponding 30 founding card identities.

Proposed price: $9.99 or a reconciled Aether equivalent.

Support two distinct purchase purposes:

### Complete my collection

Grant only eligible missing card quantities toward the target collection.

Display exact missing contents and calculated price before confirmation.

### Purchase an additional set

Grant one additional playable copy of every card included in the collection.

Clearly disclose that this grants additional copies of already-owned cards.

Do not confuse these products with recommended 15-card battle decks.
