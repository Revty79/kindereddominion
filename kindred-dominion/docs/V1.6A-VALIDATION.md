## Security and economy findings

New administrative APIs require the existing authenticated administrator role and CSRF protection. Write routes retain existing rate limits and strict request schemas. Ordinary players cannot preview, activate or apply grants. The client cannot choose additional cards, amounts or an arbitrary configuration for an initial acquisition.

Exact preview digests include the selected configuration, recipe/content versions, original Kindred, full current ownership and proposed edition/quantity changes. Ownership, content or policy changes require a fresh preview. Already-granted accounts return the original receipt without issuing another acquisition.

Additional lots have origin `starter`, `tradeable=false`, the selected configuration version and recipe source reference. They use the accepted acquisition/inventory ledger and lifetime-identity machinery. Tests prove these copies cannot fund a trade and that first-copy shop protection still uses thirty lifetime unique identities. Starter expansion adds no Gold, premium currency, tradeable supply or automatic commercial grants.

The nine-profile economy comparison conserves balances and yields exactly ten additional owned copies per player, with unchanged unique-card growth, Gold, booster counts and trading supply. There remain **120 unique founders**, requiring at most **24 guaranteed-new five-card boosters**; the tutorial gift is included in that count. Additional playable copies remain separate from first-copy completion.

The owner-review field is a trusted administrator's recorded checkpoint. It does not cryptographically establish who supplied approval. Production activation is rejected. No new credential storage or external service is introduced.

## Balance results and limitations

The main study completed **4,616 matches**; a separate legal-action observer completed **240 matches** and matched thirty existing-runner signatures. All had zero faults, timeouts, invariant failures and loop safeguards. Full results, alternatives and original-deck comparisons are in [the balance report](v1.6a/BALANCE.md).

Across 400 cross-Kindred matches per deck per scoring mode, proposed Constructs won **19.75% Classic / 20.00% Domination**, while Mythical won **64.00% / 62.00%**. These are significant balance signals. They do not establish competitive parity or identify a particular card as the sole cause. No gameplay changes were made to equalize results.

All main-study hands were legally playable after the actual AI mulligan: 600/600 per Kindred per mode. Initially playable rates were 100% for Nature/Undead and 99.67% for the other three. First-player wins were 54.13% Classic and 54.93% Domination, including mirrors. Mean match lengths were 22.64 and 22.76 recorded actions, including mulligans and passes.

Observed unavailable hand instances include late crowded boards and temporary dependencies. They are not permanent dead-card counts. Evolution final-location and drawn-minus-played metrics are explicitly labeled proxies. All fifteen proposed decks independently validate ownership, opening-slot counts and evolution predecessors. AI samples are not human acceptance, exhaustive exploit analysis or a guarantee for every possible hand.

The separate unchanged-official-deck baseline completed **1,200 matches** with zero faults/timeouts/invariants/loop safeguards. Original Constructs won 21.25% Classic / 20.63% Domination across 160 cross-Kindred matches per mode; original Mythical won 78.13% / 76.25%. This reproduces the direction of the existing disparity, although different sample sizes and deck inputs do not establish a precise causal effect. Including this baseline, V1.6A balance studies completed **6,056 matches**.
