# KINDRED DOMINION
## V1.2 Supplemental Authorization — The Founding Kindreds

This supplement modifies the previously issued V1.2 development instructions concerning card library scope.

All other V1.2 requirements remain in effect.

### 1. Expanded Provisional Card Library

After implementing and validating the Developer Studio and its supporting infrastructure, expand the existing 30-card provisional development library into five complete provisional collections containing 30 unique cards each.

The resulting active development library must contain 150 unique cards.

The five Founding Kindreds are:

| Kindred | Primary identity |
|---|---|
| Nature | Expansion, cooperation, population, swarming and poison |
| Mythical | Raw power, destruction, territorial breakthroughs and resilience |
| Arcane | Manipulation, transformation, enhancement, weakening and disruption |
| Undead | Sacrifice, resurrection, replacement and death-triggered abilities |
| Constructs | Fortification, protection, concentrated influence and defense |

Each Kindred must be independently competitive in principle while offering meaningful opportunities for mixed-collection strategies.

### 2. Provisional Card Distribution

Use the following recommended distribution as a starting point for each collection:

- 12 Rank I standard cards.
- 7 Rank II standard cards.
- 4 Rank III standard cards.
- 3 replacement cards.
- 4 evolution cards.

This distribution may vary where necessary to support a Kindred's strategic identity.

Each collection must contain exactly 30 unique cards.

Document any deviations and their design rationale.

### 3. Preserve Existing Content

Preserve the original 30-card V1.1 library and its four test decks as an immutable, recoverable content version.

Reuse suitable existing card definitions in the expanded library where appropriate.

Do not unnecessarily discard working cards.

The expanded library must be a separate provisional content version that the owner can explicitly activate.

Historical saves, replays and validation scenarios must remain compatible with their original content versions.

### 4. Card Design Requirements

Every Kindred must contain:

- Effective opening cards.
- Territorial expansion options.
- Placement-token development.
- Fortification options.
- Overthrow options.
- Offensive and defensive strategies.
- Support creatures.
- Counterplay against opposing strategies.
- Replacement combinations.
- Evolution paths.
- Meaningful late-game options.

Not every Kindred should excel at every mechanic.

Card abilities should reflect the creature's identity wherever possible.

Avoid creating mechanically identical cards with different names merely to reach the required quantity.

Use only genuinely implemented and validated mechanics.

Do not introduce major new engine mechanics solely to complete the provisional library.

### 5. Evolution Requirements

Include meaningful evolution relationships within each Kindred.

Demonstrate both:

- Exact-predecessor evolution.
- Criteria-based evolution.

Support branching and multistage evolution where appropriate.

Cross-Kindred evolution may be included where it creates meaningful mixed-deck strategies without compromising single-Kindred viability.

Avoid excessive power creep or evolution paths that are unnecessarily difficult to complete.

### 6. Starting Decks

Create one recommended 15-card starting deck for each of the five Founding Kindreds.

Each starting deck must:

- Contain cards exclusively from its own Kindred.
- Comply with Standard Play restrictions.
- Be constructible from a starting collection containing one copy of each of its 30 unique cards.
- Provide sufficient opening opportunities.
- Demonstrate its Kindred's primary strategy.
- Include meaningful tactical combinations.
- Avoid excessive reliance on drawing a particular card.

Also create at least five additional mixed-Kindred test decks demonstrating different strategic combinations.

Preserve the original four V1.1 test decks within the baseline content version.

### 7. Player Testing Experience

Allow the owner to select any of the five complete provisional collections.

Provide immediate access to all 150 cards for development testing, without implementing commercial ownership or purchasing systems.

Support:

- Pure single-Kindred decks.
- Mixed-Kindred decks.
- Custom deck construction.
- Standard copy restrictions.
- Unrestricted Free Play.
- Classic and Domination scoring.

The owner must be able to edit and test every new card through the Developer Studio.

### 8. Initial Balance Testing

Use the completed simulation infrastructure to evaluate the expanded library.

Conduct tests covering:

- Every Kindred against every other Kindred.
- Same-Kindred matches.
- Mixed decks against pure decks.
- Mixed decks against other mixed decks.
- Classic and Domination scoring.
- Alternating starting players.
- Extreme duplicate strategies in unrestricted Free Play.
- Evolution and replacement combinations.
- Fortification versus overthrow.
- Resurrection and potential ability loops.

Run a minimum of 100 matches per primary Kindred matchup and scoring ruleset, with balanced starting-player assignments.

Report actual results and sample sizes.

Identify potential dominant cards, ineffective cards, problematic combinations, engine faults and first-player advantages.

Do not interpret computer simulations as proof of competitive balance.

Do not automatically rebalance cards without preserving the original definitions and documenting changes.

### 9. Scope and Approval

All 150 cards remain provisional development content.

Their names, statistics, abilities, classifications, influence patterns and evolution relationships are subject to owner review and revision.

Final commercial approval will occur during V1.3.

Do not implement final artwork, booster purchasing, commercial progression, multiplayer, tournaments or payment processing.

Do not sacrifice completion or quality of the Developer Studio to accelerate card creation.

Complete the original V1.2 infrastructure requirements first, then use the resulting tools and shared validation systems to establish the expanded provisional card library.

### 10. Additional Deliverables

Include:

- Complete definitions for all 150 provisional cards.
- Five complete 30-card Kindred collections.
- Five recommended pure starting decks.
- At least five mixed-Kindred test decks.
- Evolution relationship diagrams.
- Collection specialization summaries.
- Simulation and preliminary balance reports.
- Identified balancing concerns.
- A recoverable original V1.1 content version.
- A separately activatable 150-card development content version.

The owner must be able to immediately play, inspect, edit and test the five Founding Kindreds upon completion of V1.2.

Proceed without requesting additional approval for individual provisional card designs.
