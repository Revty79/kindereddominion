## Follow a turn

Select/place a card using the existing review/direct, drag, keyboard or touch controls. While the request is pending, the server resolves your action and the opponent response. Once saved, the presentation shows your actual consequences followed by the accepted opponent action.

The **Amber · concealed hand** marker is a representative origin, never an exact hidden hand position. Watch the revealed portrait move to its destination, then follow the actual affected tiles and direction arrows. **RESOLVED** identifies accepted effects; **PREVIEW** and **PATTERN** remain tentative placement planning. T = tokens, P = effective power, F = fortification, CAP = ownership, X = destruction, SH = protection, EV = evolution and R = replacement. The displayed score settles on the saved result.

**Skip animation** returns immediately to the saved position. Focus loss, rotation/resizing, fullscreen changes, animation failure and reduced-motion mode also reconcile safely. Those transitions cannot replay an action or grant another reward. Use **Details & log → Accepted action sequence** for the full ordered text when a brief animation is difficult to read. Restore after interruption shows the latest saved battlefield rather than replaying missed visual events.

## Manual acceptance

- [ ] Begin a normal match with an existing legal deck; verify collection, artwork and saved match availability.
- [ ] Follow an opponent selection, revealed card flight, destination and final settled creature. The rest of its hand must remain hidden.
- [ ] Check actual token, ownership and fortification changes after arrival, including direction of influence.
- [ ] Read an ability source and its affected cards/tiles; compare displayed power changes with the accepted position.
- [ ] Observe evolution/replacement and identify the original and resulting creature.
- [ ] Observe destruction and a protected target; verify the removed/surviving creature and resulting board.
- [ ] Compare previous/resulting totals and highlighted scoring rows.
- [ ] Verify your placement has one initial movement followed by the same consequence language used for the opponent.
- [ ] Attempt rapid extra input during presentation; no second action should submit. Use Skip and continue normally.
- [ ] Change focus, rotate/resize, leave/reopen a match and reload after acceptance. Verify the saved state and no duplicate results.
- [ ] Enable the operating system's reduced-motion setting. Results should appear immediately without card flight.
- [ ] Test mouse and keyboard on a desktop, then native finger targeting on a physical phone and tablet, in portrait and landscape. Battlefield scrolling and clipped controls must not return.
- [ ] Complete a normal match; check its result/replay/history and ordinary progression exactly once.
- [ ] Review animation pacing and readability for owner approval. Full preference controls belong to V1.6F.


No required SHA argument, manual release approval or routine operator reinstallation is introduced. Preserve the existing [backup/recovery procedure](V1.5-OWNER-ACCEPTANCE.md#backup-and-recovery).
