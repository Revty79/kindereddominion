## Last Move

1. Begin or resume a match and accept an ordinary move.
2. Select **Last Move**. The board and scores explicitly switch to **HISTORY — NOT LIVE**. Choose **Your move** or **Opponent move**.
3. Read the played card/location and effect description. Open **Move before and after** or **Recorded effect sequence** for details. Enlarge the played card, or inspect a card at a recorded battlefield step, to read its information.
4. Use **Replay move from beginning**, **Stop replay**, **Previous step**, **Next step**, or **Final result**. Instant mode starts manual inspection at the first recorded step without animating.
5. Use **Current game** or Escape to return immediately. Closing an enlarged-card dialog with Escape keeps the move inspection available. Return focus goes back to Last Move.
6. If a newer saved move arrives, inspection returns to current play with a notice. Open Last Move again for the newer history. No inspection action submits a game move or grants progression.

An old match may say presentation was not recorded. Its current position is still playable. After a new accepted move, compatible recent details become available. Very complex/oversized history is similarly marked unavailable rather than guessed.

## Preferences

Use **Display** during a battle, or **Settings → Battle presentation** from the main menu. Choose Follow device setting, Normal, Fast, Reduced motion or Instant, adjust the implemented switches and select **Save presentation settings**. Settings belong to the logged-in account and follow it across devices.

An explicit Normal choice can enable standard movement even when the device requests reduced motion. Follow device setting remains the default. If another device saved first, reload its saved settings before choosing again. The former device-local checkbox is not automatically copied into another player's account.
