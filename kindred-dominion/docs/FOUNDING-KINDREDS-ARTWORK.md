# Kindred Dominion — Founding Kindreds Artwork Manifest

**Scope:** 150 owner-designed collectible cards (five Kindreds, 30 cards each).  
**Source:** Owner-design V1 roster supplied to Cody in V1.3B/C. Not the original V1.1 or Cody-authored V1.2 provisional library.  
**Status:** Creature names and artwork concepts are provisional pending review; gameplay balance is handled separately. This is an artwork reference, not an executable card definition file.

## Artwork production standards

- Produce one original, individual portrait illustration per card, preferably 3:4 aspect ratio. No collage, card border, text, number, statistics, logo, or ability diagram in the image.
- Match the realistic fantasy style established in the first Nature illustrations: recognizable subjects, detailed textures, cinematic lighting, clear silhouettes, and readable composition at small card sizes.
- Use the creature’s natural or thematic identity to suggest its role. Evolution forms should retain recognizable visual features from their predecessors.
- The suggested master filenames below use permanent uppercase collectible numbers (e.g. `N001.png`). Preserve source masters separately from optimized production assets.
- Use the existing Developer Studio publishing pipeline to approve artwork and produce the final revisioned runtime filenames and asset manifest. Do not manually overwrite immutable published assets.
- Official packaged artwork must be committed with its references; unpublished artwork remains backed up separately.
- Generate original artwork rather than copying existing game characters or designs. Names and branding remain subject to commercial clearance.
- Artwork briefs are creative suggestions, not changes to gameplay or the approved creature roster.

## Production and review workflow

1. Generate artwork in batches of three, using the exact card numbers and creature names in this manifest.
2. Inspect each image for subject accuracy, composition, consistency and unwanted text before saving it under its card number.
3. Import and assign the image through Developer Studio. Keep the original master.
4. Approve the artwork, export the publication package and use the existing repository importer and asset validation.
5. Commit the published definitions, manifest, optimized assets and corresponding masters. Verify the cards in the game.

Never renumber cards or repurpose a number for a different creature without an explicit owner decision before publication. Gameplay changes and artwork revisions do not change a collectible number.
