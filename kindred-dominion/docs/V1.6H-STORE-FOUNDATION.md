## Resulting storefront

Card Shop opens a marketplace landing page with seven department links, a featured area, current authoritative available balances and clear navigation to packs, history and collection. Departments reuse the existing product classifications:

| Department | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Booster Packs | General and Kindred-specific first-copy/additional boosters; published premium, collector and future-expansion classifications share existing slot/pool logic. Randomized delivery is labeled explicitly. |
| Complete Collections | Missing-first-copy completion, explicit additional sets, deluxe classifications and reusable expansion pools. Completion prices reflect the actual eligible contents. |
| Individual Cards | Search by creature name/number/collection, distinguish first versus additional copies, inspect quantities and lifetime eligibility. |
| Artwork Gallery | Existing alternate-art licenses and multi-artwork products; themed/evolution-chain/collector selections use explicit existing cosmetic IDs rather than inventing playable cards. |
| Customization | Department and product classification are prepared. Non-alternate-art cosmetics remain inactive until their rendering/equipment support is accepted; the store explicitly explains its empty state and cannot sell unusable items. |
| Featured Products | Only published explicit highlights or offer-classified products. Published discounts compare the same exact eligible contents before/after the configured discount. No invented MSRP, countdown or scarcity. |
| Aether Exchange | Existing commercial component filtered to exact Aether packages, existing sandbox confirmations/Checkout/orders/fulfillment/history and clear store return. Ordinary accounts cannot obtain sandbox grants. |

Product cards display artwork or a labeled development cover, name, description, fixed/randomized contents, exact currency/price, copy classification, relevant rarity, associated collections, owned quantities, eligibility, schedules and limits. Product details expose the existing exact identity list and conditional slot probabilities. A focused modal supports keyboard trapping, Escape and returning to the opener. Choosing Review retains the existing separate purchase confirmation; this is independent of immediate **gameplay** placement.

Search, Kindred/rarity/collection/ownership/copy/currency/availability filters and sorting operate across the catalog before twenty-product pagination. Search stays visible; advanced controls are grouped under **Filters & sorting** to keep products accessible on phones. Price sorting groups currencies before comparing amounts; Gold and Aether are not treated as interchangeable units. Reset clears the actual submitted search as well as the visible field. Loading updates disable stale product actions, and late/aborted loads cannot replace newer results.

Pending shop confirmations retain their original account-specific recovery key and gain automatic focus/scroll. A synchronous lock prevents overlapping quote/purchase clicks. Interrupted reveals continue using saved server results; revealing never grants cards again. A saved commercial-order indicator links to the existing order workflow. Pausing the shop retains history and independent sandbox Checkout guards.

## Reused and extended systems

`Storefront.tsx`, `StoreProduct.tsx`, `storefront.css` and the shared department classifier supply presentation. `Shop.tsx` preserves the accepted quote/purchase/reveal/history paths. `Commerce.tsx` reuses the product card and adds an optional Aether-only catalog view; its saved Checkout, provider destination checks, fulfillment and financial-history behavior remain in place.

`storefront-catalog.ts` extends the existing catalog projection. The listing reuses `offer` with one server timestamp and aggregated account purchase counts, avoiding a SQL round trip per displayed/filtered product. Quote creation and settlement continue their original player locks, independent authoritative availability checks, current configuration/fingerprint checks and atomic wallet/acquisition/receipt transaction.

The existing shop schema adds presentation-only `featured` and `edition` fields with nonpromotional defaults for old documents. Configuration drafts, publication, version history, role checks, identity semantics and rollback are reused. No new configuration, wallet, inventory, purchase, pack-opening, product-identity or payment service is introduced. Old pending confirmation terms may conservatively require a fresh review when their parsed product shape changes; completed receipts and idempotent settled requests remain immutable.

The product artwork route authenticates the account, checks published/sandbox visibility and the selected configuration version, and reuses immutable approval/checksum/format validation. Unpublished, archived, foreign-sandbox and stale-version previews are refused. Historical purchase labels and configuration references remain visible after product retirement. [Artwork requirements and publication/backup steps](V1.6H-PRODUCT-ARTWORK.md) describe the existing source/runtime registry contract and owner-supplied artwork.

## Defects found and corrected

- The previous product list ignored its already-validated product artwork references. Protected previews and a meaningful fallback now render them.
- Old category navigation separated additional boosters/cards from the relevant shopping departments. Department classification combines acquisition mode with actual delivery type while retaining explicit first/additional distinctions.
- Whole-catalog sorting could otherwise introduce hundreds of repeated timestamp/limit reads. An actual PostgreSQL query-count regression verifies bounded listing reads while settlement still checks authoritative limits.
- A product modal lost its opener when the loading state disabled that button. The opener is captured before loading and restored after closing, without stealing focus from a purchase confirmation.
- Native select sizing extended the phone document by 11 pixels. Scoped responsive filter grids and explicit sizing remove horizontal overflow while retaining ordinary vertical store scrolling.
- The new cosmetic browser fixture initially queried ownership before purchase completion. It now waits for the actual saved-purchase status; required ownership and ledger assertions remain unchanged.
- The first exact-commit Ubuntu run exposed an overstrict test-only port check: native isolated PostgreSQL uses a separate loopback port, while the network-isolated CI container uses internal port 5432. The storefront integration suite now relies on the established verified integration manifest, role and private instance token, as the other suites do. Normal DEV/hosted targets remain refused. No production service, updater safeguard or pinned workflow was changed for this correction.

## Administration and repeat checks

Administrators use the existing Shop configuration draft editor. A complete product can specify `featured: true` and `edition` of `standard`, `premium`, `collector`, `deluxe` or `expansion`; these do not alter mechanics, rarity probabilities, contents or prices. Preserve `status`, `enabled`, optional UTC schedule, exact contents, copy mode, currency/price and purchase limits. Validate and publish using the accepted workflow. Ordinary players/developers remain unable to publish administrator configuration. Never create a featured badge for an unpublished product or enable unsupported cosmetics merely to populate a department.

The existing TypeScript/unit/database/build/asset/isolation commands remain valid. There is no standalone ESLint command. The added acceptance project and compiled journey are:

```powershell
node node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.acceptance.json
node node_modules/tsx/dist/cli.mjs scripts/validate-v16-browser.mts --env .local/v1.3d/kd_test_<registered-id>.env --only=storefront
```

Build backend/player/control output first, and prepare the explicitly registered disposable database with the existing isolation/migration/seed tools. The storefront group uses its dedicated loopback ports 3102/5273 and provider **simulation**; the guarded runner refuses normal DEV and hosted targets before starting services. All genuine Stripe keys stay owner-controlled and ignored. This milestone does not require changing those keys or configuring products at Stripe manually.
