# V1.3B/C owner guide

The default for new ordinary matches is **The Founding Kindreds — Owner Design V1**: your 150 supplied designs, numbered N001–N030, M001–M030, A001–A030, U001–U030 and C001–C030. The numbers, lowercase gameplay IDs and artwork identities remain stable when names, artwork or balance change. All cards are freely available for development; no commercial ownership or purchases are simulated.

## Start on Windows

Double-click `Start Kindred Dominion.cmd` in the application folder, or run:

```powershell
cd D:\kindereddominion\kindred-dominion
npm ci
npm run dev
```

Open **http://127.0.0.1:5173/** and keep the terminal open. Stop with Ctrl+C. Use the same browser and address for local saves. Browser-local saves are specific to the device, browser and origin; `localhost`, `127.0.0.1` and a home-server address do not share them.

New installs expose the owner collection immediately. An existing workspace receives a separate owner version and switches the default for new matches once; its working draft, older versions, scenarios, reports and replays remain intact. A resumed match keeps its recorded definitions and artwork. After installation, explicitly selecting a historical version stays selected until changed again.

## Play or inspect the five Kindreds

Select one of the five supplied pure decks: **Wildwood Assembly**, **Legendary Host**, **Elemental Conclave**, **Restless Legion** or **Iron Bastion**. Each contains exactly the supplied 15 distinct cards. The five additional mixed decks demonstrate population/auras, protected legendary creatures, sacrifice/Construct history and other combinations. They are development suggestions, not changes to the supplied starters.

Choose Classic or Domination and Computer or local two-player. For custom decks, choose **Build deck**. Standard normally permits two copies, with three for Field Mouse; Free Play allows a full 15-copy deck while keeping all effect safeguards. The collection restriction in play-format options controls whether a deck must be pure or may mix Kindreds.

**Card library** displays numbers, names, printed power, rank, placement type, classifications, influence, requirements, set, expansion and edition. Search an exact number such as `N027` to find that card. Filter by Kindred, set, power, placement, classification or actual mechanics. Number sorting uses numeric serials, including numbers above 999; names and illustrations are not identities. Placeholders stay separate from text, statistics, borders and diagrams.

## Follow a turn

- Hand badges show the count of legal positions or explain why a card needs territory, tokens, a friendly body or a predecessor. Select a card to highlight real legal placements.
- Select a legal position before confirming. The preview lists actual immediate territory, token, fortification, power, aura, dynamic, protection and lifecycle consequences, with the immediate score change. Pattern markers show influence coverage; the consequence list shows what actually happens.
- A preview stops at the first private draw or at the end of the immediate action. It does not reveal future draw identities or later private consequences. Automatic turn transfer resolves after confirmation.
- Both row powers, the row winner and awarded row points are distinct. The right-hand row summaries remain visible while horizontally scrolling a small screen. Domination counts appear beneath their battlefield columns. The scoreboard separates winning rows, creature points and card bonuses.
- A battlefield creature's number is available in its hand/details; current effective power is distinguished from printed power. Click a creature for its rules, permanent changes, auras, dynamic contributions, protections and sources. Detailed score calculations are expandable.

Open **Learning and action playback** for Fast, Normal, Slow, Step through or Instant presentation. Playback displays actual captured engine snapshots while the complete committed action is already saved. **Next resolution step** advances the presentation; **Skip presentation** returns to the committed field. These controls never execute another game action or change the result. Clicking a creature during playback pauses automatic playback for read-only inspection.

The event log is collapsed by default. Private draw identities are hidden in player-facing explanations. Developer exports with both hands are available only in Studio-enabled builds. Local handoffs continue to conceal the next player's hand until explicitly revealed.

## Nine prepared owner experiments

Open **Developer Studio → Battlefield Sandbox → Load saved scenario** and choose an `OWNER` experiment. The original 30 LAB experiments remain alongside them. Prepared owner experiments use exactly the owner catalog, with no additional collectible designs or special driver cards.

| Experiment | Execute and observe |
|---|---|
| Honeybee | Place Honeybee at A1, then Player Two's Air Elemental at A2. Wolf Cub changes 2 → 3 → 2 as the live grant arrives and leaves. |
| Wolf lineage | Evolve the B1 Cub into Grey Wolf, let Player Two pass, then evolve to Alpha Wolf. Inspect population scaling versus the outgoing form's aura. |
| Basilisk | Replace Basilisk at B1 with Flytrap. Cyclops regains its two lost power; Flytrap uses the captured sacrifice power. |
| Pegasus / Sphinx | Replace Pegasus at B1 with Sphinx and open Score breakdown. Row power and the separate three-point bonus remain distinct. |
| Lesser Mimic | Replace the enhanced Red Fox at B1 with Flytrap. Mimic's calculated bonus shrinks without changing its permanent modifier. |
| Bone Hound | Replace it at B1 with Corpse Amalgam. The incoming +1 gift, captured +2 sacrifice growth and Ghoul's single allied-death reaction are separate effects. |
| Living Shield | Player Two plays Crocodile at A2; direct destruction is prevented. Player One can still sacrifice the Shield to Flytrap. |
| Phoenix, blocked | Cerberus replaces the A2 Whelp and destroys the weakened Phoenix. Its buried Firebird is not a living predecessor, so resurrection fails. |
| Phoenix, supported | The same action with another Firebird at B1 permits one resurrection by evolution, keeping the prepared weakening and lineage limits. |

Select **Execute scenario**, choose the active hand card, click its legal destination, then **Execute placement at …**. **Edit construction** unlocks setup; execution preserves the original construction. Select diagnostic snapshots to inspect intermediate steps read-only. Save or duplicate explicitly before editing a supplied experiment.

All 150 individual-card test cases are separately validated with existing-mechanic development drivers. Those drivers appear only in test fixtures and do not become collectible cards. The committed [individual validation receipt](v1.3bc/INDIVIDUAL-CARD-VALIDATION.json) identifies their actions and verified replay signatures.

## Save, edit, restore and publish

Export **Developer Studio → Content Versions and Backups → Export complete Studio backup** before significant work. Browser storage is not the only backup. Complete backups preserve drafts, versions, scenarios, reports, replays and experimental artwork. Imports validate the whole candidate before committing it atomically; stale tabs cannot overwrite newer revisions.

To design from the owner baseline, restore **The Founding Kindreds — Owner Design V1** as a new draft. Edit the working copy, create a new checkpoint and explicitly activate that checkpoint for new games. Historical versions and active matches are not edited. The original 30, Cody's provisional 150 and V1.3A laboratory remain selectable through development content controls.

Changing executable statistics/rules through Save card increments the gameplay definition revision. Display-name and illustration edits retain gameplay identity. Duplicate creates an unnumbered experimental draft; it does not allocate or reuse an official collectible number. Assign future official numbers by the reviewed registry process in the [architecture record](V1.3BC-ARCHITECTURE.md), retaining retired entries. Never edit the frozen owner V1 definitions to rebalance them in place.

New owner content uses engine **1.3.1**, content schema **2**. Engine 1.1.0 and 1.3.0 records remain supported with their historical definitions. The sole added numerical capability is reading the selected ruleset's fortification maximum for C016/C020; it does not introduce another mechanic family. See [Master Card Mechanics](MASTER-CARD-MECHANICS.md).

## Artwork handoff

Use the [150-entry artwork production manifest](v1.3bc/ARTWORK-PRODUCTION-MANIFEST.json). It lists number, stable artwork ID, subject, classifications, Kindred and evolution relations. Creature illustrations remain separate from card frames, numbers, rules, power and diagrams. No final illustrations were generated in this stage.

Import source artwork into Studio under the existing stable ID (`n001`, etc.). Keep original masters separate, use optimized WebP for production creatures, and explicitly approve/export the associated content and artwork package. Import it using the [existing portable publication workflow](V1.2-ARTWORK.md). Commit definitions, manifest, original sources and optimized revisions together. Historical versions retain their original references; changing a picture never changes a gameplay ID or collectible number.

The tester build defaults to placeholders. To share a later approved illustration version, publish it into the repository with its own content-version ID and select that ID with `VITE_PLAYER_CONTENT_ID` during `npm run build:player` (or `KINDRED_CONTENT_ID` for Docker). The build rejects local image data and unapproved creature assets. The only testing-level image exceptions are the existing neutral UI fallback and crest; temporary Briar Sprout art is excluded.

## Balance and next steps

Read the [preliminary balance report](v1.3bc/PRELIMINARY-BALANCE.md) and [owner-review issues](V1.3BC-OWNER-REVIEW.md). The exact supplied decks and all numerical statistics are retained. Computer results are engineering evidence, not proof of human competitive balance. In particular, the supplied single-Firebird starter cannot normally supply a second living Firebird for Phoenix's resurrection; the separate two-Firebird experiment does not replace that starter.

For invited players, build with `npm run build:player`. [Docker preparation](V1.3BC-DEPLOYMENT.md) is ready for a later authorized installation; Docker execution remains pending owner validation on this machine. The [PostgreSQL/storage proposal](V1.3BC-STORAGE.md) is design only. There are no accounts, online multiplayer, purchases, authoritative server ownership or cross-device progression yet.

For feedback, send the owner the seed, mode, card numbers, screenshot, expected result and actual result through an external channel. Do not treat local game data as proof of ownership or competitive results. Final artwork, server installation, database-backed players and multiplayer require their separately authorized stages.
