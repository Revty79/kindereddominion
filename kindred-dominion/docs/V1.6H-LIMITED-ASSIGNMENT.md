# Kindred Dominion — V1.6H Supplement
## Limited Edition Collectibles, Serialized Ownership and Global Supply Controls

Repository: `Revty79/kindereddominion`

The owner has approved adding genuine limited-edition collectible capabilities to the commercial store.

Incorporate this assignment into V1.6H.5–8. Preserve the V1.6A–G gameplay release checkpoint.

## 1. Supported collectible types

Implement reusable limited-edition capabilities for:

- Gameplay cards.
- Alternate artwork.
- Card backs.
- Battlefield appearances.
- Card treatments.
- Future cosmetic effects.
- Future collectible bundles.

Do not hardcode limited-edition functionality exclusively to gameplay cards.

## 2. Edition definitions

Support administrator-configurable:

- Permanent edition identifier.
- Edition name.
- Description.
- Underlying gameplay or cosmetic identity.
- Maximum lifetime issuance.
- Optional individual serial numbering.
- Product artwork.
- Release date.
- Availability window.
- Acquisition methods.
- Purchase price.
- Currency.
- Per-account purchase limits.
- Reserved reward allocations.
- Tradeability.
- Competitive eligibility.
- Publication status.

Support any valid positive supply limit, including one-of-a-kind editions.

## 3. Immutable supply guarantees

Once an edition is published, its advertised maximum lifetime issuance must become immutable.

Do not allow ordinary administrators to increase published edition limits.

Use separate edition identities for subsequent releases.

All acquisition methods must draw from the appropriate edition supply, including:

- Shop purchases.
- Tournament rewards.
- Achievements.
- Promotional grants.
- Administrator-authorized grants.
- Future redemption codes.

Prevent issuance beyond the configured maximum.

Do not silently replenish sold-out editions.

## 4. Serialized ownership

Support unique collectible instances for individually numbered editions.

Each instance must retain:

- Permanent edition identity.
- Unique serial number within the edition.
- Underlying gameplay or cosmetic identity.
- Original acquisition provenance.
- Issuance timestamp.
- Current owner.
- Transfer history.
- Relevant commercial transaction references.
- Current restrictions and status.

A serial number must never be assigned to two collectible instances.

Transfers must preserve the original serial number.

Do not replace existing permanent gameplay card IDs with edition serial numbers.

Maintain compatibility with quantity-based gameplay ownership.

An alternate artwork collectible must not grant an additional playable card unless explicitly included in the purchased product.

## 5. Global inventory and transaction integrity

Implement server-authoritative global issuance accounting.

Support:

- Maximum supply.
- Issued quantity.
- Available quantity.
- Reserved allocations.
- Pending purchase reservations.
- Completed fulfillment.
- Expired reservation release.
- Sold-out status.

Use PostgreSQL transactional locking or equivalent safe atomic mechanisms.

Concurrent purchases, reward claims and administrator grants must never exceed the edition limit.

Payment retries and duplicate events must not create duplicate collectible instances.

## 6. Refunds and retired instances

Preserve immutable issuance and serial history.

Propose an explicit policy for refunded, revoked, surrendered or otherwise retired limited-edition instances.

Do not automatically recycle serial numbers or secretly increase edition supply.

Keep historical issuance counts distinguishable from currently active ownership.

Integrate with existing refund, dispute and acquisition provenance safeguards.

## 9. Trading compatibility

Implement configurable cosmetic tradeability while preserving existing ordinary card trading safeguards.

Default new limited-edition cosmetic trading to disabled until explicitly configured.

Players must be able to identify the exact serialized collectible involved in a trade.

Do not substitute another copy of the same edition without renewed consent.

Preserve complete transfer histories.

Do not enable real-money player-to-player transactions, cash withdrawal or auction services.

Unique limited-edition gameplay cards must follow configurable deck-based competitive eligibility rules.

Preserve Founders tournament restrictions.

Rarity, serial number and collectible scarcity must not directly modify gameplay statistics.
