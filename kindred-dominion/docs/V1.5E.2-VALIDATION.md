## Implementation

Additive migration eleven, `1796300000000_trade_settlement.cjs`, adds private block records as a safety prerequisite, offer heads, immutable exact versions, durable events, settlement/transfer ledgers and idempotent requests. Head participants and monotone revisions are protected. Deferred database constraints require submitted consent and bilateral settlement to commit together; an accepted-but-unsettled state cannot commit. Historical versions/events/transfers/requests cannot be edited or deleted.

`trades.ts`, `trade-support.ts` and `trade-routes.ts` implement private draft/revision/submission, direct offers, counteroffers, recipient acceptance, author cancellation, recipient decline, server expiration, histories and own ownership/deck projections. Each submitted version is the author's exact consent; a counteroffer reverses author/recipient and requires new opposite consent. Superseded reservations release atomically. Drafts and their unsubmitted revisions remain private even after cancellation or later submission. Ordinary responses expose public participant identities and the viewing player's quantities, not the counterparty's private collection totals or source-lot history.

Both sides contribute positive bounded permanent card quantities. There are no currencies, fees, cosmetics, cash, auctions, chat or subjective equal-value rules. The sender reserves its offered side; requested contents remain provisional until the recipient owns eligible copies and explicitly accepts. No public generic inventory-lock endpoint exists.

All mutation paths acquire participant locks in sorted order. Identity locks precede combined source-ancestry locks; policy selection is shared-locked while a settlement validates. Current account requirements, blocks/restrictions, source provenance, original holds, availability, exact version/expiry, reservations and limits are rechecked. Expiration cleanup is separated from the current exchange's source locking; global pause still permits cancellation/decline/expiry recovery.

The exchange consumes the **specific reserved acquisition lots**, not a FIFO selection from a broad set of eligible lots. This matters when another reservation releases extra units while an offer is pending: consuming different copies would preserve total quantities but misattribute commercial/reward provenance. The shared inventory service now accepts a server-internal exact consumption plan and verifies that every specified lot/quantity was consumed. Public clients cannot provide that plan.

Each side's outgoing acquisition and each incoming source-linked lot/transfer commit in one transaction. Net quantities are checked against the exact pre/post exchange projection. Source-lot ancestry, original references, environment and lifetime-first-acquisition history persist through further trades. Retries return the existing settlement; neither conflicting keys nor new-key repeated acceptance can grant again. Events persist atomically for the separately recoverable notification projection in E.4; no external communication provider is activated.

Saved decks retain their original contents. Preview warns about newly insufficient quantities; subsequent deck reads and match entry use authoritative ownership legality. A previously started match retains its complete state, record, content, deck and presentation snapshot and remains playable after a trade.

`trade-integrity.ts` reconciles each transfer against immutable source/destination lots, original roots, exact consumption, incoming/outgoing acquisitions, participant consent and versioned intent. It compares per-card directional quantities with the original offer and checks ancestry. Later legitimate consumption, restrictions or retirement do not falsely invalidate historical settlement. The existing CLI restoration now validates both reservations and this trade ledger, in addition to wallets, ownership, replay signatures, Studio and configuration checksums. Earlier schemas remain supported through relation-existence guards.
