## Implementation

The existing authentication, PostgreSQL, inventory, immutable content and authoritative match architecture is retained. No founding card definition, permanent ID, evolution relationship, combat statistic or game rule changed.

- `server/economy-rules.ts`: validated integer currency/reward definitions, provisional defaults, XP curves, objective schedules, shared mastery allocation and disabled competitive definitions.
- `server/configuration.ts`: reusable private drafts, optimistic revisions, validated immutable publication, integrity hashes, publication history and controlled audited rollback. Publication and audit writes share a transaction; stale selection fails closed.
- `server/economy-assets.ts`: origin-separated wallets, immutable transactions/entries, credit lots and consumption, ownership provenance and lifetime first-copy history. Reconciliation checks aggregate balances, immutable ledgers, individual lot consumption and preserved legacy anchors.
- `server/economy.ts`: atomic, idempotent composite rewards, account/mastery milestones, UTC objectives, backlog consumption, tutorial prerequisites and replay-verified match settlement. Match reward configuration is frozen at enrolment. Existing matches receive no invented historical rewards.
- `server/economy-routes.ts`: authenticated wallet/history/objective APIs, explicit promotions, protected investigation, restrictions, named grants, compensations and recovery. Original credit compensation cannot remove unrelated or already spent units.
- Existing auth, inventory, starter/deck and match services now record real economy events and provenance inside their existing transactions. The local sampler is sandbox-only. A match whose deck requires sandbox copies is permanently classified as development and earns no ordinary progression. This classification does not change an already-started match when ownership subsequently changes.
- Player Progression displays wallets/origins, account XP, Kindred mastery, tutorial steps, challenge claims and receipts. Finished matches have a compact reward summary. Protected Control adds draft editing/publication/rollback, player search, reconciliation, audited currency/bundle grants, compensation and restrictions. Published promotions are visible and claimable once; successful card rewards refresh the main collection/deck data.

`ECONOMY_ENABLED` defaults on only for local DEV/integration, off for hosted environments. Production activation is rejected. A valid active configuration must also have been published; migration does not activate defaults. Existing deployment trust policies and `sudo kindred-update` are retained.

## Simulation

Run `npx tsx scripts/economy-simulation.mts` (installed local `tsx`; no package download is needed). `scripts/economy-model.ts` exposes all engagement, win-rate, eligibility, curve and reference-price inputs. These are deterministic estimates, not observed play or actual revenue.

The reference ordinary/targeted booster prices are **250 / 300 Gold**, for modeling only in C. A player starts with 30 unique cards and needs **24 five-card guaranteed-new packs** for the remaining 120. Additional playable copies are a separate economy.

| Scenario | Hours/day × days/week | Normal matches/hour | First-copy completion hours / calendar days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual free | 0.5 × 4 | 8 | 10.38 / 36 |
| Average free | 1 × 5 | 10 | 10.00 / 12 |
| Highly active free | 3 × 6 | 10 | 17.10 / 6 |
| Competitive engagement proxy, Normal AI | 2 × 6 | 10 | 12.30 / 8 |
| Rapid surrender attempt | 4 × 7 | 60 attempted | No eligible matches, no packs |

The model assumes guide/collection/deck prerequisites and immediate valid claims, a Nature deck, and excludes speculative ability/evolution/diversity rates. Startup grants and finite level rewards make the initial collection faster. Across the full modeled year, Gold earnings including repeatable objectives correspond to roughly **32, 34, 62 and 45 minutes** per reference pack for the four legitimate scenarios. Base match rewards alone correspond to approximately **117, 85, 81 and 74 minutes**. The high-volume scenario is slower per active hour because of diminishing rewards; these differences are disclosed rather than hidden by increasing the 24-pack collection requirement.

Recommend retaining configurable DEV settings for actual playtesting. Finite onboarding rewards are intentionally front-loaded; daily wins can encourage stopping after a short session, and Gold accumulates after first-copy completion unless later approved products provide a sink. D/G must evaluate additional-copy acquisition and the complete economy. No automatic balance, rarity or commercial-policy changes follow from this simulation.

## Security and recovery

The server owns prices/rewards, eligibility, provenance and balances. Public activity acknowledgements are limited to guide/collection inspection and recorded once. They cannot assert wins, placements, abilities or XP. Tutorial grants additionally require a legal deck and eligible match. Match settlement verifies the stored replay and final-state signature. Fast surrender, testing access, sandbox/restricted accounts, cooldown and daily limits have recorded denial reasons.

Integer basis-point scaling uses BigInt division: a reproduced large-unit floating-point case previously rounded one unit upward and now has a regression assertion. Player locks serialize wallet/inventory/reward mutations; source identities and immutable receipts enforce once-only settlement. Failing composite operations roll back money, XP, cards and receipts together. CSRF, trusted origins, persistent authenticated sessions, role/ownership checks, bounded inputs, existing API limits and additional administrator-write limits apply. Sandbox designation is permanent; sandbox credits require that designation and cannot enter an ordinary spend. Administrative XP rehearsal is sandbox-only. No payment provider or live purchase is active.

If the client loses a response, refresh the match, objective, history or operation receipt before retrying its original key. Administrators can inspect `/api/admin/economy/rewards` for denied/pending decisions and `/api/admin/economy/reconciliation` for accounting differences. A justified retry uses `POST /api/admin/economy/matches/{matchId}/reconcile` with `{ "reason": "documented investigation reason" }`; it honors the original configuration and receipt. The retroactive preview endpoint never applies a historical grant. Investigate reconciliation errors before issuing an explicit audited correction.

Configuration rollback selects an immutable version; it does not reverse settled purchases, grants or XP. Currency compensation targets the original remaining credit lots. Database rollback uses the existing verified **separate-database** restore/recovery procedure; destructive down migrations and in-place restoration remain disabled.
