# Kindred Dominion — V1.6H.1–2
## Store Architecture Audit and Complete Shopping Experience

Repository: `Revty79/kindereddominion`

The owner has approved the overall commercial store design. Complete the storefront using the existing V1.5 economy, shop, acquisition and Stripe sandbox infrastructure.

## 1. Prerequisites and development boundaries

Inspect the actual accepted implementations of:

- V1.5C economy and progression.
- V1.5D shop and collectible acquisition.
- V1.5E Trading Hall.
- V1.5F Stripe sandbox.
- V1.5G economy integration.
- V1.6A–G gameplay improvements.

Complete the gameplay release checkpoint before publishing any V1.6H changes.

Keep unfinished store development isolated from the validated gameplay release.

The owner will be conducting hosted gameplay testing while V1.6H development proceeds.

Reuse existing functionality rather than replacing working systems.

Preserve all accounts, collections, decks, matches, balances, achievements, trades, commercial records and permanent card identities.

Do not automatically deploy to homeserver.

Do not enable live payments.

## 2. Existing store audit

Compare the existing application against the complete approved store direction.

Classify each required capability as:

- Complete and reusable.
- Implemented but requiring improvement.
- Partially implemented.
- Missing.
- Blocked by owner-supplied artwork or external credentials.

Document findings and correct actual deficiencies.

Do not create duplicate wallet, ownership, purchase, pack-opening, product or payment services.

## 3. Seven store departments

Implement or refine these departments:

### Booster Packs

General founding boosters, Kindred-specific boosters, premium boosters, collector boosters and future expansion packs.

### Complete Collections

Standard collections, deluxe collections, collection-completion products, additional-copy bundles and future expansions.

### Individual Cards

Searchable eligible gameplay card catalog with first-copy and additional-copy purchase options.

### Artwork Gallery

Individual alternate artwork, themed artwork collections, evolution-chain artwork bundles and collector editions.

### Customization

Card backs, battlefield appearances, decorative card treatments and future presentation cosmetics.

### Featured Products

New releases, promoted products, genuine discounts, seasonal content and special collections.

### Aether Exchange

Premium currency packages and secure Stripe sandbox checkout.

All departments must use the existing authoritative product and transaction architecture.

## 4. Store navigation

Create a coherent, attractive storefront.

Provide:

- Store landing page.
- Featured product area.
- Department navigation.
- Gold and Aether balances.
- Product search.
- Relevant filters and sorting.
- Product details.
- Ownership indicators.
- Purchase history.
- Pending purchase indicators.
- Clear return navigation.

The interface should feel like a collectible fantasy card game marketplace, not a generic administrative product table.

Preserve the established artwork-first visual identity.

## 5. Product cards

Create a reusable product presentation component.

Support:

- Product artwork.
- Product name.
- Product description.
- Exact contents or randomized content description.
- Price and currency.
- Ownership eligibility.
- Collection association.
- Rarity information where relevant.
- Promotional status.
- Availability.
- Purchase limits.
- Product preview.
- Purchase action.

Fixed-content and randomized products must be clearly distinguishable.

Do not use artificial urgency, misleading discounts or fake scarcity.

## 6. Shopping accessibility

Support desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile browser layouts.

Provide:

- Responsive product grids.
- Readable pricing.
- Keyboard navigation.
- Touchscreen controls.
- Accessible descriptions.
- Meaningful loading states.
- Clear empty states.
- Useful purchase rejection messages.
- Reduced-motion compatibility.

Normal scrolling is appropriate within the store.

Do not extend the battlefield's no-primary-scroll requirement to unrelated shopping pages.

## 7. Artwork preparation

Create reusable product artwork references compatible with the existing asset publication pipeline.

The owner will supply original commercial artwork separately.

Support temporary clearly identified development placeholders when official artwork is unavailable.

Do not replace official creature illustrations.

Do not copy third-party commercial game packaging or branding.

Do not make unavailable artwork a blocker for developing functional store infrastructure.

## 8. Product visibility

Support:

- Permanent inventory.
- Scheduled products.
- Featured products.
- Unpublished products.
- Disabled products.
- Archived products.
- Account-specific eligibility.

Unpublished and unauthorized products must not be obtainable through direct API requests.

Archived products must remain visible in relevant historical purchase records.
